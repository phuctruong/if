  - Observed: 10-100× more bright galaxies than expected

- **Flattened reionization timeline** (Curtis-Lake et al. 2023)
  - ΛCDM predicts: Sharp transition ~100 Myr after Big Bang
  - Observed: Gradual transition, uncertain timeline

**Severity Assessment:** These results directly contradict the gradualist formation timeline predicted by ΛCDM. Mainstream cosmology now faces a "crisis" (multiple papers explicitly use this language).
