## The decisive question

**Q1 (existence):** In the sealed mobility regime, do PRODUCERS exist — structures that
emit ≥ 2 eventually-mobile child tracks during their life? If yes, selection has a unit
of heredity to act on and the tracking program reopens. If no, ballistic one-shot movers
are the ceiling of this rule family, and the honest conclusion is that B3/S23-class
energy-gated Life cannot produce tracking agents — the program must change rule family
or close this branch.

**Q2 (resource coupling, contrast):** Is the mover-production rate different between the
gradient arm (σ=40) and the gradient-free placebo (σ=10⁶)?

## Pre-committed protocol (FROZEN before any run)

### Definitions

- **P1 production event**: a new track born at t>0 whose birth cells, dilated by
  radius 2, overlap the t−1 cells of ≥ 1 live track. Parent = the track with maximal
  overlap. Newborns with no such overlap are *spontaneous* (soup condensation), not
  production.
- **P2 mover production**: a production event whose child track eventually classifies
  mobile under the sealed D3 (life ≥ 40, disp ≥ 8, size ≤ 60).
- **P3 producer**: a track with ≥ 2 mover-production events during its tracked life
  (≥ 2 excludes one-off fission debris).

### Design

- Universe: sealed regime `B3/S23, e_birth=0.25, e_maint=0.01, inflow=12, ρ=0.15`,
  600 steps. **Roster: seeds 7, 11, 23, 42, 101, 202, 303, 404, 501–516 (24 seeds),
  both arms (σ=40 and σ=10⁶), declared here.**
- **Q1 frozen verdict** (gradient arm): mean producers per run ≥ 0.5 → **PRODUCERS
  REPRODUCIBLE**; total ≥ 1 but mean < 0.5 → **RARE** (reported, no upgrade);
  total = 0 → **ABSENT** (the ceiling conclusion fires).
- **Q2 frozen verdict** (only if Q1 ≠ ABSENT): Welch t on per-run mover-production
  counts, gradient vs placebo. ±2 thresholds, else undecided.
- **Instrument control**: in the C1 seeded-glider universe (empty world), the lone
  glider must register ZERO production events (nothing to fission from) — guards
  against attribution false-positives from re-labeling noise.
