## What IF Theory is

A research program testing whether **one logarithmic equation, normalized by
the prime number theorem, can stand against the dark sector of the universe
with no new free parameters**. The core proposal:

```
Φ(r) = 1 / log(r/r₀ + 1)
C_XI = 62                  (from the Mersenne tower observation: 2 × π(127) = 2 × 31)
r₀   = 0.6595 kpc          (from σ₈ = 0.8111 via the Peebles §36 integral)
```

The headline empirical result: r₀ = 0.6595 kpc agrees with the empirically-fit
galaxy correlation scale of ≈ 0.65 kpc to ~1.5%, with **zero new free
parameters** on top of the standard cosmological background.
