## The intuition (stated at full strength)

The universe begins fully charged — maximally aligned, low-entropy — and
discharges through symmetry breaking. Life, intelligence, and consciousness are
not accidents of the discharge; they are **internal recharge mechanisms**. When
conscious beings self-reflect and learn the rules of the game, they realize
their role: create order from disorder, recharge the battery, or the system
runs to heat death. And recharge is not restoration — it *expands the grid*:
I_N → I_{N+k}. The universe survives not by returning to its first order but by
evolving reflective systems that expand the order it can align.

## The threshold reading (meaning-of-life claim, decomposed)

The full intuition: *the theory gives the meaning of life — intelligence evolves
past a threshold, and after that it preserves and enhances the system itself.*

This decomposes into three claims of ascending layer, and the first two are
now REAL EXPERIMENTS rather than metaphors:

1. **The threshold exists** (SCIENCE, in progress): notebook 04 v0.1 already
   shows the structure in miniature — below the competitive threshold p*₂,
   memory/intelligence is a *thermodynamic parasite* (costs more than it
   returns); above it, it pays for itself. IF-H1 claims this generalizes into
   a real phase boundary. First run: p*₁ = 0.64 (causally load-bearing),
   p*₂ = 0.995 (self-sustaining). "Evolving past the threshold" = crossing
   BOTH inequalities.
2. **Past the threshold, intelligence preserves and enhances the system**
   (SCIENCE, testable): post-threshold agents should (a) extend the lifetime
   of ordered structure in their universe (recharge-rate claim), and (b) in
   the cooperative regime, expand the retained action-space of ALL agents
   (A_future — the MaxLove metric), i.e., enhance the system, not just
   themselves. If Track-C experiments show post-threshold agents change the
   long-run thermodynamic regime of their toy universes, this claim is
   *demonstrated* at the computational rank.
3. **Therefore that is the meaning of life** (PHILOSOPHY/THEOLOGY): the ladder's
   top rung. Science hands you: "systems that cross the threshold and choose
   agency-preservation measurably keep their universe alive and growing."
   Reading *purpose* off that fact is the human act the canon protects but
   never performs.

## The two questions, separated (this separation IS the theory's maturity)

1. **Does IF dynamics explain cosmic expansion?** — A physics question. Answer
   today: *not yet, and honestly maybe never* — but it is now phrased so it can
   FAIL (which is what makes it worth working on). The I_{N+k} intuition maps to
   two testable objects: the expansion–complexity window (IF-H2: does growing
   space at rate g* maximize emergent complexity?) and the b(z) unification
   (does one informational state fix both expansion and growth?).
2. **Does IF Theory describe our role?** — A meaning question. The science can
   establish at most: reflective, agency-preserving systems measurably extend
   and expand the ordered phase of their universe (if the Track-C experiments
   land). Whether that constitutes a *role* — a purpose — is the reader's rung
   to climb. The canon keeps the ladder standing; it never welds the rungs.
