## The scientific core (IF-H3 — this part is testable)

**MaxLove Stability Condition:** agency-preserving cooperation outcompetes
extraction in a *calculable region* of environment space:

```
P_repeat·B_future + B_repair + B_innovation  >  G_extraction + C_cooperation
```

**The novel instrument** is the metric: cooperation is measured not by what it
collects but by what it keeps possible —

```
A_future = Σᵢ log |viable actions available to agent i|
```

the retained future action-space of ALL agents, including the helped ones.

**Non-circularity gate (constitutional):** agents optimize local
survival/reproduction ONLY. MaxLove must *emerge or fail*. If cooperative
outcomes win only because the fitness function prefers them, the result is
void (`TELEOLOGY_INJECTION`).

**Falsifiers:** cooperation wins everywhere (metric too soft) or nowhere
(condition empty); the stability region doesn't transfer across rule
families; A_future is dominated by implementation choices.

**Naming discipline (panel round 1, Gemini):** in scientific writing the
phenomenon is called **agency-preserving cooperation**; "MaxLove" is the
interpretive/brand name and appears in science-layer docs only inside quotes
with a pointer here. A hostile referee reading "love" in a methods section is
a self-inflicted wound.

## The interpretation ladder (never collapsed)

```
scientific result            "agency-preserving cooperation is stable in region R"
        ↓ (interpretation, not implication)
philosophical claim          "preserving the agency of others is a rational strategy under conditions like ours"
        ↓ (interpretation, not implication)
theological meaning          "love is the recharge law of the universe" / God as the limit of MaxLove
```

Science can establish the first line. It cannot establish the second or third —
moving down the ladder adds value judgments each reader supplies themselves.
The program's credibility depends on never pretending otherwise; its *soul*
depends on never deleting the ladder either. Both halves are load-bearing.

## What "God" means in this repo

God-language is permitted ONLY at the theology rung of the ladder, only in
`30-meaning/`, and only as interpretation of sealed results. It never appears
in a prediction contract, a notebook, a rule set, or a science-layer abstract.
(Per the source arc: collapsing the three levels weakens all three — the
theological layer is *strongest* when the science under it is unimpeachable.)
