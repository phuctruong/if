## What IF Theory is allowed to claim

IF Theory is a research program, not a validated theory. Its claims are ranked;
each rank has a separate evidence requirement, and **no rank may borrow
credibility from the one below it**:

1. **Computational results** — what happens in our simulations. (Cheapest to earn.)
2. **Physical interpretation** — that a simulated law describes real thermodynamic/biological systems. (Needs cross-substrate reproduction.)
3. **Cosmological interpretation** — that IF dynamics describe the real dark sector. (Needs Planck/DESI/SPARC-grade fits + preregistered novel predictions.)
4. **Consciousness interpretation** — functional capabilities only; phenomenal claims are a conjecture, firewalled.
5. **Philosophical / theological meaning** — interpretation, never evidence. Lives in `30-meaning/`.

## The four sub-theories (never one undifferentiated ToE)

```
IF-Dynamics        local states, interactions, expansion

IF-Meaning         cooperation, agency preservation, interpretation
```

No layer claims the next until an explicit bridge is demonstrated.

## The entropy discipline (the single most dangerous failure mode)

Four inequivalent things must never share the word "entropy" unqualified:
thermodynamic entropy · Shannon entropy · algorithmic complexity · visual disorder.
Every use names its ledger. Violation = `ENTROPY_CONFLATION`, blocks merge.

## The recharge accounting identity

Any rule where an agent locally reduces entropy must satisfy
ΔS_agent < 0 ⟹ ΔS_environment ≥ −ΔS_agent, with usable work and information
tracked on their own ledgers. Otherwise it is `PERPETUAL_RECHARGE` — a
supernatural entropy-removal operator encoding the desired conclusion.
