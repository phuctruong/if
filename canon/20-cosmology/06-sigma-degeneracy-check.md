# The Σ-Degeneracy / RAR Check — family A's wedge is closed by existing data

> Auth: 65537 · Layer: SCIENCE · Written 2026-07-18 (loop iteration 8).
> This is the literature check that `05-lif-family-selection.md` §3 demanded before any
> estimator could be frozen. It touches no kinematics of ours; it reads published
> results. The verdict is a kill.

## The bound

Family A's distinctive, MOND-forbidden prediction was: rotation curves depend on
informational *structure* beyond the baryonic acceleration profile — i.e., systematic
residuals from the radial acceleration relation (RAR) correlated with structural order
at fixed g_bar.

Published measurements on the same SPARC data our admission bar uses:

- **McGaugh, Lelli & Schombert 2016 (PRL 117, 201101):** the RAR g_obs(g_bar) holds
  across 153 galaxies with **0.13 dex** observed scatter.
- **Lelli, McGaugh, Schombert & Pawlowski 2017 (ApJ 836, 152, "One Law to Rule Them
  All"):** residuals are Gaussian with ≈ **0.11 dex** width, largely accounted for by
  observational uncertainty, and — the lethal part — **show no correlation with galaxy
  properties** (baryonic mass, gas fraction, radius, surface brightness, and the other
  properties tested).
- **Intrinsic-scatter follow-ups (e.g., Li et al. 2018 A&A; Li et al., arXiv:1908.06105):**
  after fitting per-galaxy M/L within priors, the intrinsic RAR scatter is consistent
  with ≈ 0 (bounded to a few hundredths of a dex).

## Verdict — FAMILY A IS DEAD (wedge closed)

Any structure term in family A must produce galaxy-level RAR residuals. The residual
budget available for it is ≲ 0.05 dex and uncorrelated with everything measured. So:

- Either the structure estimator's effect is **below detectability in SPARC** — then
  family A cannot distinguish itself from MOND on the only dataset the frozen admission
  bar admits, and its "distinctive content" is unfalsifiable there
  (`INTERPOLATION_SMUGGLING` with extra steps);
- or it predicts residuals above that bound — then it is **already falsified** by
  Lelli et al. 2017.

There is no third position. Family A joins family C in the kill log — total data cost
of both kills: zero new fits.

## Falsifiability of this document

This check dies if: a published reanalysis finds RAR residuals correlated with a
structural property at > 0.05 dex; or SPARC-quality data on a structure-extreme pair
(identical Σ(r), maximally different azimuthal order) shows curve differences. Either
would reopen family A exactly as specified in `05-lif-family-selection.md` §3.
