# Entry 08 Survey — Cluster-Merger Memory: PARKED on a data gap

> Auth: 65537 · Layer: SCIENCE · Written 2026-07-18 (loop iteration 13).
> The lattice-hardening queue asked: do published samples support a τ_IF prereg
> (Δx_lens(t) = Δx₀ e^(−t/τ_IF) across a merger population)? Answer below. No fits run.

## What exists

- **Collision ages:** the Merging Cluster Collaboration (Golovich et al. 2017–2019)
  provides a homogeneous radio-relic-selected sample of **29 merging clusters** with
  dynamical modeling; typical observation epoch ≈ 1 Gyr after first pericenter, with
  per-system time-since-pericenter (TSP) constraints derived from relic positions.
- **Offsets:** Harvey et al. 2015 measured DM–galaxy offsets for **72 substructures in
  30 systems**. However, **Wittman et al. 2018 identified substantial errors in those
  offset measurements** and relaxed the derived SIDM bound from σ/m < 0.47 to
  ≲ 2 cm²/g, further noting that many systems violate the analysis assumptions.

## Verdict — PARKED (data gap, per the entry's designed off-ramp)

A τ_IF prereg needs per-system (offset, TSP) PAIRS with credible errors. Today:
1. The offset sample and the TSP sample are different samples with unknown, likely
   modest overlap.
2. The offset measurements themselves are unreliable at the individual-system level
   (the Wittman critique) — a decaying-offset regression on ~10 noisy pairs has no
   discriminating power and would invite a fishing verdict.

Per the frozen queue ("if not, record the data gap and park"): **entry 08 is parked.**

## Reopen conditions (specific, so future sessions don't re-litigate)

Reopen if ANY of: (a) a homogeneous weak-lensing re-measurement of the MCC radio-relic
sample publishes per-system offsets with errors (Euclid-era lensing is the natural
source); (b) a published cross-matched table of ≥ 10 systems with both offset and TSP
appears; (c) a simulation-calibrated forward model makes population-level (not
per-system) offset-decay testable against existing heterogeneous data — that variant
would need its own prereg with the systematics model frozen first.
