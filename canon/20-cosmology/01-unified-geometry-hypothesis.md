## The claim (exact form — nothing weaker is novel)

The phenomena attributed to dark matter and dark energy are **two regimes of
one underlying nonequilibrium informational–geometric dynamics**:

```
localized informational strain      →  dark-matter-like attraction   (clustering mode)
global informational discharge      →  dark-energy-like expansion    (relaxation mode)
```

with a **fixed quantitative relation** between them. Not "no dark matter" —
rather: no dark-matter *particles* and no independent dark-energy *substance*.
Anything that gravitates like dark matter can be called effective dark matter;
the claim is that it is transient geometry, and that this difference is
observable.
