# IF Theory: Scope, Definitions, and a Falsification Framework

## A Scientific Constitution for the Informational Field / Informational Battery Research Program

**Author:** Phuc Vinh Truong

## Abstract

IF Theory is a proposed computational and mathematical research program investigating whether a common physical and informational framework can describe transitions from nonequilibrium capacity to persistent structure, self-maintaining life, predictive agency, reflection, and cooperation. A more speculative branch asks whether related dynamics could contribute to emergent spacetime, apparent missing gravity, or cosmic expansion.

This paper does not present IF Theory as a validated theory of physics, consciousness, cosmology, morality, or everything. Its purpose is constitutional: to define the theory’s terms, distinguish scientific claims from philosophical and theological interpretations, identify the closest prior research, state the theory’s possible novelty, and establish conditions under which its central hypotheses must be rejected.

The foundational proposal is that an **informational battery** should not be treated as a new mystical substance or as energy created by information. It is provisionally defined as a system’s physically accessible nonequilibrium capacity together with the structured correlations that determine how that capacity can be used. IF Theory will maintain separate energy, thermodynamic-entropy, and information ledgers. It will not add bits directly to joules or treat visual disorder as thermodynamic entropy.

The program’s strongest candidate contribution is the **IF Causal-Work Principle**: internally maintained information becomes constitutive of agency only when interventionally preserving that information enables more useful physical work or future viability than the complete cost of acquiring, storing, updating, and using it. This proposition will be tested through deterministic simulations, model ablations, parameter sweeps, synthetic recovery, and later cross-substrate experiments.

The paper defines an explicit hierarchy of claims, from computational results through possible physical, biological, cosmological, consciousness-related, philosophical, and theological interpretations. Each level carries a separate evidence requirement. The program succeeds only if its conclusions survive simpler baselines, intervention tests, preregistration, independent reproduction, and meaningful opportunities for failure.

# 1. Introduction

Simple local systems can produce unexpectedly rich macroscopic behavior. Cellular automata demonstrate that deterministic local transitions can generate persistent objects, movement, replication, and computation. More recent neural cellular-automaton systems have demonstrated learned growth and regeneration from decentralized local interactions. These results establish that complex-looking organization need not be explicitly controlled at the global level, but they do not establish that the physical universe is literally a cellular automaton. citeturn252506search1turn252506search2

Nonequilibrium thermodynamics likewise demonstrates that entropy-producing systems can generate and sustain ordered structures when supplied by appropriate flows of matter or energy. Prigogine’s work on dissipative structures showed that irreversible processes far from equilibrium can support organized states rather than producing only featureless decay. Such organization does not violate the second law: local order is maintained through exchanges with a larger environment and accompanying entropy production. citeturn985462search1turn985462search5

Information is also physically embodied. Landauer argued that logically irreversible computation is associated with physical dissipation, and later experiments demonstrated the expected thermodynamic bound in controlled one-bit systems. This does not mean that every bit has a fixed energy or that abstract information is itself a free source of power. It means that physical operations involving information have implementation-dependent energetic and entropic consequences. citeturn262952search0turn262952search1

Research has further attempted to distinguish information that merely correlates with an environment from information that contributes to a system’s continued existence. Kolchinsky and Wolpert developed an intervention-based definition of semantic information for autonomous nonequilibrium systems: correlations count as meaningful to the system when removing them damages its viability. Causal-emergence research has separately shown that, under specified causal measures, a macroscopic description can sometimes possess greater effective causal organization than a microscopic description. citeturn866367search0turn866367search1

IF Theory enters this existing landscape. Its foundational ingredients are therefore not individually new. Its possible contribution is a specific **cross-scale research program**:

\[
\boxed{
\text{nonequilibrium capacity}
\rightarrow
\text{persistent organization}
\rightarrow
\text{self-maintenance}
\rightarrow
\text{predictive agency}
\rightarrow
\text{reflection}
\rightarrow
\text{cooperation}
}
\]

The program asks whether these transitions can be measured under one consistent resource-and-information accounting system, without inserting life, agency, consciousness, morality, or cosmic purpose into the primitive rules.

A speculative second branch asks whether a related informational state could constrain emergent geometry, apparent missing gravity, and cosmic expansion. That branch begins with a much higher evidential burden. The standard cosmological model remains highly successful across the final Planck temperature, polarization, and lensing measurements, while DESI DR2 has provided precise baryon-acoustic-oscillation measurements from more than fourteen million galaxies and quasars. Any IF cosmology must reproduce the successful predictions of existing cosmology before claiming to replace its dark sector. citeturn866367academia51turn866367search3

This paper establishes the rules under which those questions may be investigated.

\[
\boxed{
\text{Can one explicit physical and informational accounting framework}
\atop
\text{connect the emergence of structure, life, agency, reflection,}
\atop
\text{and cooperation without placing those outcomes in the primitive rules?}
}
\]

A secondary, substantially more speculative question is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Can any successful IF law be extended to make unique quantitative}
\atop
\text{predictions about spacetime, gravity, cosmic growth, or expansion?}
}
\]

The first question can be studied through artificial universes, deterministic simulations, causal interventions, and potentially laboratory systems.

The second requires contact with established fundamental physics and observational data. It cannot be answered by visual resemblance between an expanding simulation and the observed universe.

# 4. Prior Research and the Novelty Boundary

## 4.1 Cellular automata and computational universes

Conway-style cellular automata establish that small local rule sets can produce complex persistent patterns. Computational interpretations of physics go substantially further, proposing that deterministic discrete substrates might underlie or reproduce aspects of quantum theory. Gerard ’t Hooft’s cellular-automaton interpretation is one developed example, including attempts to relate deterministic ontological states to quantum descriptions. citeturn252506search1turn252506academia34

IF Theory therefore cannot claim novelty merely for proposing that the universe may be computational, discrete, graph-based, or locally updated.

Its stricter requirement is to demonstrate that a minimal substrate produces specific cross-scale transitions under explicit thermodynamic and intervention-based accounting.

---

## 4.2 Nonequilibrium order and dissipative structures

The proposition that order can emerge in entropy-producing open systems is established in nonequilibrium thermodynamics. Dissipative structures remain organized through continued exchanges with their environments, and biological nonequilibrium physics studies how living systems maintain organization through driven processes. citeturn985462search1turn262952search6

IF Theory therefore cannot claim novelty for stating that life preserves local order while exporting entropy.

Its possible contribution is a quantitative account of when persistent organization begins using internally maintained predictive information to alter its own resource access and viability.

---

## 4.3 Information thermodynamics

Landauer’s principle and subsequent theoretical and experimental work establish a relationship between logically irreversible information processing and thermodynamic cost. Information-bearing physical systems must obey ordinary physical laws; computation does not grant exemption from energy conservation or entropy production. citeturn262952search0turn262952search1turn262952search5

IF Theory therefore rejects any rule in which reflection, intelligence, or observation freely removes entropy.

The complete cost of sensing, storage, error correction, communication, prediction, action selection, and erasure must be included in any claim that information creates an adaptive advantage.

---

## 4.4 Nonequilibrium free energy and relative entropy

In defined isothermal stochastic settings, the departure of a probability distribution from equilibrium can be related to a free-energy difference through relative entropy. This relationship provides a mathematically disciplined starting point for the informational-battery concept, but it does not justify applying one formula universally across arbitrary cosmological, biological, and computational systems. citeturn262952academia35turn262952academia37

IF Theory therefore treats nonequilibrium free energy as one possible implementation of physical battery capacity, not as a final universal definition.

---

## 4.5 Semantic information and autonomous agency

Intervention-based semantic-information frameworks already ask which correlations matter to an autonomous system’s viability. This is a direct precedent for IF Theory’s attempt to distinguish irrelevant stored information from information that causally supports continued organization. citeturn866367search0

The potentially new IF question is whether semantic information has a measurable **net causal-work value** after the full physical cost of maintaining and using the information is deducted—and whether a common dimensionless threshold predicts agency across different substrates.

---

## 4.6 Causal emergence

Causal-emergence frameworks quantify cases in which an appropriately selected macrolevel possesses stronger effective causal structure than the underlying microlevel. This provides a possible method for identifying organisms or agents without assuming that the programmer’s preferred spatial grouping is the correct one. citeturn866367search1turn866367search6

IF Theory therefore cannot claim that “the whole becomes more than the parts” as an original discovery.

It must identify precise coarse-grainings, interventions, and held-out predictions under which a detected macrostructure has explanatory or causal advantages.

---

## 4.7 Active inference and the free-energy principle

The free-energy principle and active inference provide formal accounts of self-organization, inference, action, and systems that appear to maintain characteristic states. Some formulations explicitly connect counterfactual beliefs, future planning, and self-evidencing behavior. citeturn985462academia25turn985462academia26

IF Theory therefore cannot claim novelty merely for describing organisms as systems that infer environmental conditions or preserve viable states.

A successful IF theory must show what its causal-work measure predicts that active-inference or viability-based frameworks do not, and it must compare the frameworks directly.

---

## 4.8 Consciousness theories

Integrated information theory, global neuronal workspace theory, recurrent-processing theories, and other approaches already propose relationships between information processing and consciousness. A large preregistered adversarial collaboration published in 2025 directly compared key predictions of integrated-information and global-workspace theories, finding a mixed pattern that challenged important elements of both. The result illustrates that broad compatibility with neural data is insufficient; theories need predictions that differ from those of their competitors before the experiment is conducted. citeturn866367search2

IF Theory therefore does not presently claim to explain subjective experience.

Its initial consciousness-related objective is restricted to the emergence and causal necessity of particular **functional capabilities**, including global information sharing, counterfactual modeling, self-representation, policy competition, and policy revision.

---

## 4.9 Cosmology and the dark sector

The final Planck analysis found strong consistency with the spatially flat six-parameter \(\Lambda\)CDM model and separately constrained baryonic and cold-dark-matter densities. DESI DR2 has sharpened measurements of the cosmic expansion history; combined inferences concerning evolving dark energy remain sensitive to model parametrization and companion datasets. citeturn866367academia51turn866367search3turn866367academia49

IF Theory therefore cannot reject dark matter or dark energy simply because an information-based alternative is philosophically attractive.

A credible IF alternative must reproduce or improve upon:

- cosmic microwave background temperature and polarization spectra;
- CMB gravitational lensing;
- baryon acoustic oscillations;
- primordial element constraints;
- galactic and cluster dynamics;
- gravitational lensing;
- large-scale structure growth;
- weak lensing;
- cluster mergers;
- gravitational-wave propagation;
- and the measured expansion history.

The individual components of IF Theory are not novel.

The potentially novel contribution is:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{gathered}

\]

A stronger, unproven extension asks whether the same underlying state could constrain both emergent gravitational response and cosmic expansion.

This novelty claim remains provisional until a comprehensive literature review and working mathematical model demonstrate that the exact formulation has not previously been published.

# 5. Formal Substrate

## 5.1 Universe state

The minimal IF artificial universe is represented as:

\[
\mathcal U_t=
\left(
G_t,X_t,\varepsilon_t,\Lambda_t
\right),
\]

where:

- \(G_t=(V_t,E_t)\) is a directed or undirected causal-interaction graph;
- \(X_t=\{x_i(t)\}\) is the collection of local internal states;
- \(\varepsilon_t=\{\varepsilon_i(t)\}\) is the local physical-resource distribution;
- \(\Lambda_t\) specifies environmental boundary conditions or external reservoirs.

The dynamics are described by a transition law:

\[
P_\theta
\left(
\mathcal U_{t+1}\mid \mathcal U_t
\right),
\]

or, for a deterministic model:

\[
\mathcal U_{t+1}
=
T_\theta(\mathcal U_t).
\]

The primitive rules may include:

- state transitions;
- local transport;
- interaction costs;
- noise;
- resource conversion;
- node or edge creation;
- node or edge destruction.

The primitive rules may not contain variables whose names or functions directly encode:

- life;
- organisms;
- intelligence;
- agency;
- reflection;
- consciousness;
- morality;
- cooperation bonuses;
- love;
- divinity;
- cosmic purpose.

These may appear only as higher-level classifications supported by operational tests.

---

## 5.2 Locality

An update is locally governed when the next state of an element depends only on a formally specified causal neighborhood:

\[
x_i(t+1)
=
T_i
\left[
x_i(t),
\{x_j(t):j\in\mathcal N_i(t)\},
\varepsilon_i(t)
\right].
\]

The neighborhood may change through lawful graph dynamics, but it may not be retrospectively altered to produce a desired outcome.

---

## 5.3 Open and closed systems

Each experiment must state whether the simulated universe is:

- energetically closed;
- open to an external resource reservoir;
- open to matter exchange;
- driven by boundary conditions;
- or expanding through a defined resource conversion.

No claim of self-recharge is valid unless the source and destination of every relevant energy and entropy flow are included.

# 6. The Three-Ledger Requirement

IF Theory requires three separate ledgers.

## 6.1 Energy ledger

For a designated system:

\[
\Delta U
=
Q_{\mathrm{in}}
-
Q_{\mathrm{out}}
+
W_{\mathrm{in}}
-
W_{\mathrm{out}}
+
\Delta U_{\mathrm{created\ or\ removed}},
\]

where the final term must be zero unless the model explicitly includes a lawful conversion with another accounted field.

Every implementation must test its energy balance numerically.

---

## 6.2 Thermodynamic-entropy ledger

For a system plus its environment:

\[
\Delta S_{\mathrm{total}}
=
\Delta S_{\mathrm{system}}
+
\Delta S_{\mathrm{environment}}
\geq 0
\]

for processes to which the ordinary second-law framework applies.

Local entropy reduction is permitted only when compensated by entropy production, heat flow, resource consumption, or another explicitly modeled process.

---

## 6.3 Information ledger

Relevant information measures may include:

### Shannon entropy

\[
H(X)
=
-\sum_x p(x)\log p(x).
\]

### Mutual information

\[
I(X;Y)
=
\sum_{x,y}
p(x,y)
\log
\frac{p(x,y)}
{p(x)p(y)}.
\]

### Conditional mutual information

\[
I(X;Y\mid Z).
\]

### Relative entropy

\[
D_{\mathrm{KL}}(p\parallel q)
=
\sum_x p(x)
\log
\frac{p(x)}{q(x)}.
\]

### Algorithmic complexity

\[
K(x),
\]

under an explicitly stated approximation or compression method.

### Effective or causal information

A specified intervention-based quantity measuring how selectively states cause later states.

These quantities are not interchangeable.

No paper may refer to “entropy” without specifying which entropy, probability distribution, ensemble, coarse-graining, units, and estimator are intended.

# 7. Definitions

## 7.1 Information

**Information** is a family of formally defined quantities describing distinctions, probabilities, correlations, compressibility, causal influence, or encoded content.

IF Theory does not define information as:

- knowledge in general;
- goodness;
- intelligence;
- consciousness;
- energy;
- complexity;
- order;
- meaning;
- or divine intention.

Each use must specify the relevant mathematical definition.

---

## 7.2 Thermodynamic entropy

Thermodynamic entropy is a physical state quantity defined within an applicable thermodynamic or statistical-mechanical framework.

It is not synonymous with:

- visual messiness;
- randomness in ordinary speech;
- algorithmic incompressibility;
- moral decline;
- ignorance;
- or information loss in every context.

---

## 7.3 Informational battery

An **informational battery** is provisionally defined as:

> A physically embodied system’s accessible capacity to produce future work or preserve viable organization because it is away from equilibrium and contains exploitable correlations.

It is represented as a structured object:

\[
\mathcal B
=
\left(
B_{\mathrm{physical}},
I_{\mathrm{accessible}},
\mathcal M_{\mathrm{conversion}}
\right),
\]

where:

- \(B_{\mathrm{physical}}\) is available nonequilibrium physical capacity;
- \(I_{\mathrm{accessible}}\) represents correlations accessible to the system’s mechanisms;
- \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{conversion}}\) specifies the physical mechanism by which capacity can be used.

In restricted isothermal systems, one possible measure of nonequilibrium capacity is:

\[
B_{\mathrm{physical}}
=
F[p]-F[p_{\mathrm{eq}}],
\]

which may be proportional under specified conditions to:

\[
k_{\mathrm B}T
D_{\mathrm{KL}}
\left(
p\parallel p_{\mathrm{eq}}
\right).
\]

This equation is not assumed to apply unchanged to every IF model.

---

## 7.4 Battery discharge

**Discharge** is the conversion of accessible nonequilibrium capacity into:

- mechanical work;
- chemical transformation;
- computation;
- transport;
- structural maintenance;
- reproduction;
- environmental modification;
- or dissipated heat.

Discharge does not necessarily mean simple decay. It may create new persistent organization while reducing another accessible gradient.

---

## 7.5 Battery recharge

**Recharge** is the restoration of accessible nonequilibrium capacity through an accounted physical process.

Recharge may occur through:

- external energy input;
- resource concentration;
- gradient creation;
- improved access to an existing resource;
- error correction;
- repair;
- reorganization;
- or coupling to another reservoir.

Intelligence may improve the efficiency with which a system locates or uses capacity. It may not create energy without a corresponding physical source.

---

## 7.6 Structure

A subset \(A_t\subseteq\mathcal U_t\) qualifies as a persistent **structure** when it demonstrates, over a specified interval:

1. temporal persistence;
2. an identifiable boundary or causal partition;
3. stronger or more functionally significant internal interactions than arbitrary comparison partitions;
4. reproducible responses to perturbation;
5. persistence under component turnover or movement, where applicable.

Visual recognizability alone is insufficient.

---

## 7.7 Life

A structure qualifies as minimally **living** within an IF experiment when it:

1. maintains a boundary or operational identity;
2. uses external gradients or resources;
3. maintains a restricted viability distribution;
4. repairs at least some disturbances;
5. preserves organization despite component turnover;
6. propagates or reproduces some portion of its organization.

This is an operational artificial-life definition, not a claim to have solved the philosophical definition of life.

---

## 7.8 Viability

Let \(Y_t\) denote variables essential to a structure’s continued organization.

A viability region is:

\[
\mathcal V
=
\{Y: Y\text{ remains compatible with continued structure}\}.
\]

Possible viability measures include:

- probability of remaining in \(\mathcal V\);
- expected survival time;
- expected future accessible work;
- return time after perturbation;
- organizational persistence;
- or a preregistered combination.

The measure must be chosen before comparing models.

---

## 7.9 Memory

**Memory** is a physically instantiated internal state whose present value carries information about prior states.

Memory alone does not establish agency or reflection.

---

## 7.10 Predictive information

**Predictive information** is internal information about future environmental or system states:

\[
I(M_t;E_{t+\tau}\mid E_t),
\]

or another explicitly defined predictive measure.

Predictive correlation alone does not prove causal use.

---

## 7.11 Causally useful information

Information is **causally useful** when an intervention that selectively removes or scrambles the information reduces a preregistered outcome while preserving relevant alternative properties.

Required comparison conditions may include:

1. intact model;
2. erased model;
3. scrambled model;
4. temporally displaced model;
5. equal-capacity irrelevant model;
6. false predictive model.

---

## 7.12 Causal-work value

The preliminary IF causal-work quantity is:

\[
\mathcal W_C(\tau)
=
W_{\mathrm{intact}}(\tau)
-
W_{\mathrm{scrambled}}(\tau)
-
C_{\mathrm{model}}(\tau),
\]

where:

- \(W_{\mathrm{intact}}\) is work obtained with the predictive model intact;
- \(W_{\mathrm{scrambled}}\) is work obtained after destroying the relevant predictive relationship;
- \(C_{\mathrm{model}}\) is the complete physical cost of acquiring, storing, updating, protecting, and using the model.

A viability version may be defined separately:

\[
\mathcal V_C(\tau)
=
V_{\mathrm{intact}}(\tau)
-
V_{\mathrm{scrambled}}(\tau)
-
C_V(\tau).
\]

Work and viability may not be combined into one scalar unless dimensional and normative justification is supplied.

---

## 7.13 Agency

A bounded structure displays **agency** when internally maintained information causally changes its action selection in a way that improves a preregistered future outcome across more than one environmental condition.

Agency is graded.

### Agency ladder

| Level | Operational capability |
|---|---|
| A0 | Persistence without adaptive feedback |
| A1 | Reactive feedback |
| A2 | Memory-dependent action |
| A3 | Prediction of future environmental states |
| A4 | Counterfactual evaluation of multiple actions |
| A5 | Self-state modeling |
| A6 | Policy revision after model failure |
| A7 | Modeling other agents |
| A8 | Building persistent cooperative institutions |

A system must pass an intervention test before receiving a level.

---

## 7.14 Reflection

**Reflection** is the capacity of an agent to:

1. represent aspects of its own state or policy;
2. represent at least two possible future courses;
3. select among them using expected consequences;
4. revise the policy when predictions fail.

Reflection is not a primitive numerical variable.

---

## 7.15 Functional consciousness

Within IF Theory, **functional consciousness** is a provisional label for an integrated, temporally extended, counterfactual control architecture that:

- represents self and environment;
- coordinates information across competing policies;
- evaluates alternative futures;
- makes selected information broadly available to relevant subsystems;
- revises behavior following prediction error.

This definition concerns measurable function.

---

## 7.16 Phenomenal consciousness

**Phenomenal consciousness** refers to subjective experience: that there is something it is like to be the system.

IF Theory presently has no validated derivation of phenomenal consciousness.

Functional performance may be necessary, sufficient, correlated, or unrelated to subjective experience. That question remains open.

---

## 7.17 Emergent space

Space is **emergent** only when effective distance, locality, dimension, and propagation structure can be recovered from underlying causal relations rather than assumed as an unexplained fixed lattice.

Possible operational distance measures include:

- minimum propagation time;
- graph geodesic cost;
- diffusion distance;
- correlation decay;
- causal-cone overlap.

Multiple independent measures should converge before the model is interpreted geometrically.

---

## 7.18 Expansion

An IF universe exhibits **effective expansion** when distances between persistent comoving structures increase according to a defined emergent metric:

\[
a_{\mathrm{IF}}(t)
=
\frac{
\langle d_{ij}(t)\rangle
}{
\langle d_{ij}(t_0)\rangle
}.
\]

Appending empty cells around an array does not by itself constitute an explanation of cosmic expansion.

---

## 7.19 MaxLove

**MaxLove** is a normative and evolutionary policy hypothesis:

> Prefer policies that preserve or increase the durable, freely exercisable agency of self and others while minimizing unnecessary coercion, destruction, and irreversible loss of future possibility.

MaxLove is not introduced as:

- a primitive physical force;
- a thermodynamic law;
- a synonym for fitness;
- or a conclusion guaranteed by the scoring function.

Scientific IF experiments may test when MaxLove-like strategies outperform exploitation. The moral value assigned to those outcomes requires an additional philosophical premise.

# 8. Claim Hierarchy

IF Theory separates seven levels of claim.

## Level C0 — Computational claims

Example:

> A specified rule set produces a stable phase transition in a deterministic simulation.

Evidence required:

- executable code;
- fixed configurations;
- invariant tests;
- parameter sweeps;
- independent reproduction.

---

## Level C1 — General physical claims

Example:

> One dimensionless causal-work threshold applies across multiple physical substrates.

Evidence required:

- mathematical derivation;
- controlled laboratory systems;
- cross-substrate scaling;
- successful prior prediction.

---

## Level C2 — Biological claims

Example:

> The IF threshold predicts transitions in living cells or organisms.

Evidence required:

- biological measurements;
- intervention or ablation;
- comparison with established biological models;
- replication across systems.

---

## Level C3 — Cosmological claims

Example:

> One IF state explains apparent missing gravity and accelerated expansion.

Evidence required:

- covariant or otherwise physically consistent equations;
- stability;
- standard limits;
- CMB, lensing, structure-growth, galaxy, cluster, and expansion fits;
- at least one distinctive prospective prediction.

---

## Level C4 — Functional-consciousness claims

Example:

> Counterfactual global coordination is necessary for a specified conscious-like capability.

Evidence required:

- discriminating computational and neurological predictions;
- preregistered experiments;
- comparison with competing consciousness theories.

---

## Level C5 — Phenomenal-consciousness claims

Example:

> A particular IF organization is sufficient for subjective experience.

Evidence required:

- presently unresolved;
- cannot be inferred solely from behavioral complexity.

---

## Level C6 — Philosophical claims

Example:

> Agency-preserving cooperation offers a defensible account of meaning.

Evidence required:

- explicit premises;
- logical argument;
- engagement with competing philosophical positions.

---

## Level C7 — Theological interpretation

Example:

> A universe capable of freely chosen, agency-preserving love is consistent with divine purpose.

Evidence required:

- theological and philosophical argument;
- may be informed by science but is not established by simulation.

A result at one level does not automatically establish a result at a higher level.

# 9. Core Hypotheses

## H1 — Accessible Battery Hypothesis

Two systems with similar gross energy and ordinary information measures may possess different physically usable capacity because their correlations differ in accessibility to actual mechanisms.

### Falsifier

After controlling for known thermodynamic variables and mechanisms, the proposed accessibility measure adds no predictive value for obtainable work or viability.

---

## H2 — IF Causal-Work Principle

Predictive information becomes constitutive of agency when its interventionally measured benefit exceeds its full maintenance and use cost:

\[
\mathcal W_C>0.
\]

### Strong form

A common dimensionless threshold predicts agency onset across multiple rule families and physical substrates.

### Falsifier

No robust threshold exists, or each substrate requires unrelated definitions and freely selected parameters.

---

## H3 — Emergent-Structure Hypothesis

Resource-conserving local IF rules can produce persistent, bounded, perturbation-resistant structures without explicitly encoding the target structures.

### Falsifier

Persistent structures occur only when their form, fitness, or repair behavior is explicitly embedded in the rule or training objective.

---

## H4 — Expansion–Complexity Window

Persistent complexity is maximized at an intermediate rate of costly causal-space growth:

\[
g_{\min}<g^*<g_{\max}.
\]

### Falsifier

The effect is absent, strictly monotonic, measure-dependent, or disappears under alternative rule families and complexity measures.

---

## H5 — Agency-Threshold Hypothesis

The transition from reactive feedback to predictive agency exhibits measurable critical behavior as predictive benefit exceeds model cost.

Possible signatures include:

- abrupt onset;
- scaling;
- hysteresis;
- critical slowing;
- increased intervention susceptibility.

### Falsifier

No shared transition behavior is found, or the apparent threshold is a direct artifact of the chosen reward function.

---

## H6 — Unified-Geometry Hypothesis

Dark-matter-like gravitational attraction and dark-energy-like expansion may be two regimes of one nonequilibrium informational geometry.

### Required consequence

The model may not independently tune galactic attraction and cosmological expansion.

### Falsifier

Separate functions, couplings, or histories are required to fit galaxies, lensing, the CMB, cosmic growth, and expansion.

---

## H7 — Functional-Consciousness Hypothesis

Integrated counterfactual self-and-environment modeling becomes causally necessary when local reactive controllers face unresolved competition involving delayed or hidden consequences.

### Falsifier

Purely local reactive architectures achieve equivalent performance under the specified conditions, or the global model can be ablated without selective loss of the predicted functions.

---

## H8 — MaxLove Stability Hypothesis

Agency-preserving cooperation becomes evolutionarily stable within a calculable region governed by repeated interaction, repair benefits, reputation, partner choice, innovation, and the cost of exploitation.

### Falsifier

The strategy succeeds only when agency preservation is directly embedded in the fitness reward, or no transferable stability condition exists.

# 10. Global Failure Conditions

The overall IF research program is severely weakened if any of the following persist after reasonable revision:

1. **Metaphor dependence:** central variables cannot be operationally measured.
2. **Free recharge:** information or reflection reduces entropy without accounted physical cost.
3. **Designer leakage:** desired macroscopic outcomes are encoded in primitive rules or reward functions.
4. **Coarse-graining arbitrariness:** emergence appears only under a chosen representation and disappears under reasonable alternatives.
5. **Parameter proliferation:** each phenomenon requires new independent parameters.
6. **No cross-scale continuity:** measures useful in toy universes have no relation to physical, biological, or observational systems.
7. **No unique predictions:** IF fits observations only after flexible retrospective adjustment.
8. **Baseline failure:** simpler established models predict the data equally well or better.
9. **No standard limit:** proposed fundamental dynamics cannot recover successful established physics.
10. **Irreproducibility:** results depend on undocumented code, unstable numerical choices, or selective reporting.
11. **Interpretive inflation:** simulation results are repeatedly presented as evidence for consciousness, morality, or theology without the required bridge.
12. **Immunity to rejection:** every failed prediction is redefined as support for another version of IF Theory.

A research program that cannot die cannot become scientifically credible.

# 11. Computational Methodology

## 11.1 Deterministic reference implementations

Every central result must have a canonical CPU implementation with:

- locked software environment;
- fixed random seeds where randomness is used;
- stable thread settings;
- documented numerical tolerances;
- versioned input data;
- checksums;
- machine-readable outputs.

GPU or distributed implementations may accelerate parameter sweeps but may not be the sole source of the canonical result.

---

## 11.2 Dual implementation

Every central equation should be implemented twice:

1. a transparent NumPy or pure-Python reference;
2. an independently written optimized implementation.

Agreement must be verified on randomized small cases.

---

## 11.3 Property-based tests

Tests must verify:

- conservation laws;
- state bounds;
- symmetry properties;
- reversibility where claimed;
- monotonicity where mathematically required;
- invariance under relabeling;
- known limiting cases;
- correct behavior under zero coupling.

Correlation is insufficient for claims about agency.

Required interventions include:

- memory erasure;
- memory scrambling;
- temporal scrambling;
- sensor removal;
- model-capacity matching;
- irrelevant-information substitution;
- policy freezing;
- communication removal;
- environmental permutation.

Every positive result must be compared with the strongest relevant simpler alternatives.

Possible baselines include:

## 11.6 Held-out prediction

Parameter selection and hypothesis testing must be separated.

Preferred designs include:

- training and held-out rule families;
- held-out galaxies;
- held-out cosmological redshift ranges;
- simulation-to-observation transfer;
- future-data preregistration.

## 11.7 Synthetic recovery

Before applying a model to observations:

1. simulate known parameters;
2. apply realistic noise and selection effects;
3. recover parameters;
4. test bias;
5. test uncertainty coverage;
6. quantify false-positive rates.

## 11.8 Null universes

Computational and cosmological analyses should include:

- shuffled data;
- Poisson processes;
- Gaussian random fields;
- standard-model mocks;
- wrong-mask controls;
- noise-only controls;
- intentionally misspecified models.

- preprocessing;
- exclusion rules;
- summary statistics;
- primary outcomes;
- pass thresholds;
- falsification thresholds;
- allowed robustness tests;
- git commit;
- environment hash;

AI coding agents may:

# 12. Evidence Classification

Every reported result must be labeled as one of the following.

## Demonstrated

Reproduced through code or experiment under stated conditions.

## Supported

Favored by multiple tests, but reasonable alternatives remain.

## Inconclusive

Current tests do not discriminate among hypotheses.

## Falsified under tested conditions

Failed a preregistered or otherwise decisive test within the stated model class.

## Speculative

Mathematically or conceptually proposed but not tested.

## Interpretive

A philosophical or theological reading rather than an empirical result.

No speculative claim may be described as demonstrated.

---

# 13. Scientific, Philosophical, and Theological Boundaries

IF Theory permits interaction among three domains without collapsing them.

## Scientific level

Asks:

- What dynamics occur?
- What quantities can be measured?
- Which hypotheses survive comparison?
- What observations distinguish models?

## Philosophical level

Asks:

- What constitutes identity or agency?
- What should count as meaning?
- What values follow from preserving agency?
- What interpretations best explain the scientific picture?

## Theological level

Asks:

- Is the universe purposeful?
- Is consciousness spiritually significant?
- Can freely chosen love be understood as part of creation?
- How might scientific findings relate to belief in God?

The permitted relationship is:

\[
\text{scientific evidence}
\rightarrow
\text{philosophical argument}
\rightarrow
\text{theological interpretation}.
\]

The prohibited shortcut is:

\[
\text{interesting simulation}
\Rightarrow
\text{scientific proof of God, love, or cosmic purpose}.
\]

Theological interpretation may be meaningful without being presented as laboratory evidence.

# 15. Limitations of the Present Framework

First, the informational-battery definition remains provisional. Nonequilibrium free energy, exergy, causal information, semantic information, and accessible work are related but not identical. Paper 1 must determine which mathematical object is suitable under which conditions.

Second, causal work may not produce a universal threshold. Agency may be substrate-specific, multidimensional, gradual, or dependent on multiple interacting resource constraints.

Third, simulated emergence is sensitive to coarse-graining. Structures visible to human observers may not be privileged causal units.

Fourth, physical work and viability cannot be freely combined. Viability contains model- and observer-dependent choices about which states count as continued identity.

Fifth, a classical computational substrate does not automatically reproduce quantum phenomena. Any claim that IF Theory is fundamental must confront interference, entanglement, contextuality, the Born rule, relativistic causality, and quantum field theory.

Sixth, replacing dark matter or dark energy requires much more than fitting galaxy curves or expansion distances. The same theory must satisfy early-universe, lensing, cluster, growth, and relativistic constraints.

Seventh, functional theories of consciousness may leave the existence of subjective experience unexplained.

Eighth, evolutionary stability cannot establish moral truth without additional normative premises.

These are not secondary caveats. They define the open problems the program must solve.

---

# 16. Conclusion

IF Theory begins with an intuition: the universe may be understood partly through how physical capacity is stored, discharged, reorganized, made accessible, and used by increasingly capable structures.

That intuition is scientifically valuable only after its terms are separated.

Energy is not information. Thermodynamic entropy is not visual chaos. Memory is not reflection. Prediction is not agency unless it causally changes outcomes. Functional agency is not necessarily phenomenal consciousness. Evolutionary success is not moral truth. Computational expansion is not observed cosmic expansion. Philosophical coherence is not experimental confirmation.

The disciplined form of IF Theory is therefore:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{gathered}
\text{A research program investigating whether physically embodied}\\
\text{information acquires measurable causal-work value, and whether}\\
\text{that value supports transitions from nonequilibrium structure}\\
\text{to life, agency, reflection, and cooperation.}
\end{gathered}
}
\]

The program’s first obligation is not to explain everything.

Its first obligation is to discover whether its central quantities can be defined, measured, reproduced, compared with existing frameworks, and falsified.

If they cannot, IF Theory should be revised or abandoned.

If they can—and if one common law predicts previously unknown behavior across simulations, physical systems, biological systems, or cosmological observations—then the program may earn the right to make progressively larger claims.
