Q1. The Novelty Question: IF vs. The Giants

Existing Context:

Kolchinsky-Wolpert (Semantic Info): Defines semantic information as syntactic information that maintains the existence/viability of a system.

England: Focuses on dissipative adaptation; systems structure themselves to absorb and dissipate energy.

Friston (FEP): Systems minimize variational free energy (surprise) to maintain their Markov blankets.

Hoel: Causal emergence; macro-scales can have higher effective information/causal power than micro-scales.

Vopson / Wheeler: Information as physical/mass or "it from bit." IF Theory explicitly rejects Vopson by refusing to mix bits and Joules.

Verlinde: Emergent gravity from entanglement entropy.

What is Genuinely Novel?
The theoretical space between England (blind dissipative structures) and Friston (complex predictive inference) is muddy. IF Theory’s novelty lies in its strict accounting mechanism. It does not assume information is magically useful (KW) or that a system intrinsically "wants" to minimize surprise (Friston). Instead, it demands a strict thermodynamic audit: a system only crosses the threshold into "agency" if the work extracted via that information exceeds the Landauer and metabolic costs of acquiring, storing, and computing it.

The Single Strongest Novel Claim:
The IF Causal-Work Principle via Interventional Ablation. Framing agency not as a philosophical state or a topological boundary (Markov blanket), but as a measurable, counterfactual thermodynamic delta—computable by simulating the scrambling of internal state memory and measuring the drop in extracted work minus the energy saved on computation.

Q3. The 5 Highest-Signal Falsifiable Experiments (Jupyter Simulations)

Ranked by Novelty × Feasibility. These experiments do not require a wet lab; they require rigorous computational physics.

1. The "Agency Phase Transition" Search (Rank: 1)

To Simulate: An active-matter particle simulation (like Lenia or complex Cellular Automata) embedded in a scarce-resource gradient. Gradually increase the memory capacity (bits) of the agents.

The Metric: Plot Net Viability (Resource gathered minus thermodynamic cost of memory/compute). Look for a discontinuous jump (phase transition).

What Kills the Theory: If viability scales smoothly and linearly with information capacity, the "agency-threshold phase transition" claim is false. Agency is just a gradient, not a state of matter.

2. The KW-Semantic vs. IF-Causal Divergence (Rank: 2)

To Simulate: A grid-world agent with perfect memory of its environment. We define a scenario where Kolchinsky-Wolpert "semantic information" is high (the memory accurately predicts food).

The Metric: Run the IF ablation: scramble the memory. Calculate the energy saved by not processing that memory versus the food lost.

What Kills the Theory: If the IF Causal-Work principle perfectly aligns with Kolchinsky-Wolpert in 100% of scenarios, IF Theory is just KW theory repackaged with different words. IF must prove scenarios exist where KW says "this is useful info" but IF says "this is a net-negative thermodynamic parasite."

3. The Thermodynamic Cost of Friston's FEP (Rank: 3)

To Simulate: An agent minimizing variational free energy (surprise) in a highly volatile environment.

The Metric: Track the 3 IF Ledgers. Measure the thermodynamic cost of updating the generative model vs. the work extracted by minimizing surprise.

What Kills the Theory: If minimizing surprise always inherently maximizes the Informational Battery, FEP is the fundamental principle, and IF is merely a downstream observation.

4. The "MaxLove" Spontaneous Emergence (Rank: 4)

To Simulate: Evolutionary game theory (Iterated Prisoner's Dilemma) on a spatial grid, but with strict thermodynamic costs for sensing neighbors, remembering past interactions, and acting.

The Metric: Survival duration of cooperative clusters vs. the total "Informational Battery" of the system.

What Kills the Theory: If cooperative clusters emerge but do not mathematically maximize the shared nonequilibrium capacity (IF Battery), then "MaxLove" is a false correlate to cooperation.

5. The Information Erasure (Landauer) Ablation (Rank: 5)

To Simulate: A Maxwell's Demon scenario where the demon's memory is finite.

The Metric: Intervene by erasing the demon's memory without paying the Landauer heat cost in the simulation, vs. paying it.

What Kills the Theory: If the 3-ledger accounting system fails to balance (violates the Second Law) when simulated deterministically under these ablations, the foundational ledger concept is mathematically broken.
