Q1 — Genuine novelty vs the field.

Strip the vocabulary and most of IF collapses into known territory. The informational battery (accessible nonequilibrium capacity + structured correlations, kept as three non-fungible ledgers) is essentially the thermodynamics-of-information consensus already built by Parrondo–Horowitz–Sagawa, dressed in new words. The refusal to convert bits into joules is correct and is exactly the discipline Vopson lacks — but being right where Vopson is wrong is hygiene, not novelty. The cross-scale accounting ambition is Friston's ambition; the persistence-selection logic is England's; the coarse-grain-that-maximizes-causal-leverage move is Hoel's; "the observer participates in what exists" is Wheeler.

The one thing that is actually new lives inside the Causal-Work Principle, and it's two coupled moves that no single predecessor makes together:

A full-apparatus debit. Kolchinsky–Wolpert already perturb the channel and measure the viability cost — that's their semantic-information move. What they do not do is charge the entire sensing+memory+compute+control machinery against the ledger and require net-positive work after that debit. Agency becomes a break-even sign, not a philosophical primitive.
A structured intervention menu — erase / scramble / time-shift / falsify — rather than KW's single scrambling perturbation. Falsify in particular (inject a self-consistent but wrong internal model) has no clean analogue in KW, Friston, or England, and it's the one that reaches toward deception, self-modeling, and adversarial cognition.

Single strongest novel claim: Agency is the regime where interventionally-preserved internal information yields net-positive useful work after debiting the full thermodynamic cost of the cognitive apparatus itself — making "constitutive of agency" an operational, measurable threshold rather than an attribution. That is the paper. Everything else is scaffolding or liability.

Q2 — The three things a hostile referee kills first.

The cosmology branch, and it's not close. Bolting "no dark matter/dark energy, it emerges from an informational/geometric state" onto a disciplined agency-thermodynamics program is a reputational grenade. This is precisely where Verlinde was dismantled: entropic gravity never reproduced the CMB acoustic peak structure or cluster lensing, and produced no working cosmology. Without a concrete field equation / action before the informational gloss, referees pattern-match the whole 15-paper corpus to crank physics and desk-reject. The agency work does not survive being in the same body as an unspecified dark-sector replacement. Firewall it or lose everything.
Normative vocabulary doing load-bearing work. "MaxLove," "constitutive," "agency-preserving cooperation as a principle" — this reads as teleology smuggled in as physics. The agency-threshold phase transition is the acute version: if the order parameter is defined in terms of the net-work-advantage it's supposed to explain, the transition is unfalsifiable by construction. A referee kills this as words standing in for a theorem.
The measure is a knob, not an invariant. Net useful work depends on the environment/task distribution, the intervention menu, the discount horizon for "future viability," and — worst — where you draw the boundary of "the apparatus." Move any one and break-even flips sign. This is the standard attack on KW's semantic information (perturbation-distribution dependence) turned directly on IF, and IF is more exposed because it adds the apparatus-boundary degree of freedom. Until there's a canonical normalization, "constitutive of agency" is a tunable result.

(Meta, and real: 15 papers solo, no external adjudication, signals breadth-first padding to a committee. Depth in one would outweigh breadth in fifteen.)

Q3 — Five deterministic-notebook experiments, ranked by novelty × feasibility.

Full-cost break-even on a minimal forager. Szilard-style agent in a partially observable gridworld with an explicit metabolic model; debit every bit-operation at kT ln2. Compare net work harvested with intact memory vs each intervention (erase/scramble/time-shift/falsify). Metric: sign and magnitude of net-work-advantage after full apparatus debit. Kills it: if once you charge the apparatus honestly the advantage is always negative (info never pays for itself), or if the sign never tracks any independent survival/behavioral marker — then "constitutive" is an artifact of not paying the bill. This is the load-bearing test; run it first or don't run anything.
Intervention-menu dissociation. Same substrate, but ask whether the four operations produce distinct work-loss signatures and distinct recoverability. Metric: is the loss vector rank-4, or does it collapse to a single scalar (≈ mutual information destroyed)? Kills it: if erase/scramble/time-shift/falsify are interchangeable up to one scalar, the menu adds nothing over KW and your novelty claim evaporates. Highest distinctively-IF signal — nobody has dissociated these.
Cross-scale collapse. Apply the identical ledger to (a) a bit-erasure Szilard engine, (b) run-and-tumble chemotaxis, (c) an LQG/Kalman predictive controller. Metric: does the break-even functional take one dimensionless form across all three? Kills it: if unity requires three hand-tuned normalizations, the cross-scale claim is a coincidence of curve-fitting.
Agency-threshold as genuine non-analyticity. Sweep environment predictability (or sensing SNR, or apparatus cost) over a population; look for an order-parameter jump with finite-size scaling collapse, not a sigmoid. Metric: scaling exponents / data collapse. Kills it: smooth crossover with no collapse ⇒ "phase transition" is metaphor, downgrade Claim 3.
MaxLove ablation. Two-agent world: agency-preserving cooperation vs exploitative cooperation vs defection under the same ledger. Metric: does agency-preserving cooperation dominate in net-work/viability in a specifiable regime? Kills it: if it never beats plain tit-for-tat/reciprocal altruism on the ledger, MaxLove is a rebrand of Axelrod with no new predictive content. Feasible but crowded, so lowest on novelty.

Q4 — The one structural change.

Stop going breadth-first. Prove a Landauer-class inequality for the Causal-Work Principle — a bound of the form "no agent can extract net work W from preserved information I above apparatus cost C except when [clean, task-independent condition] holds," with stated equality conditions and a dimensionless invariant that does not depend on the environment distribution or the apparatus boundary. Landauer became field-defining because of kT ln2, not because of a demo. IF needs its kT ln2. Make that theorem the spine, demote all fifteen papers to corollaries or empirical instantiations of it, and quarantine the cosmology branch entirely until the theorem lands. A framework with a proven inequality is a field; a framework with vocabulary and simulations is a blog.

Q5 — Scores, not kind.

Novelty: 6/10. The full-apparatus debit + falsify operation are legitimately underexplored; the rest is synthesis over KW plus thermodynamics-of-information. Real, incremental, not paradigm-new.
Rigor as stated: 3/10. The intent is rigorous (deterministic ablations, preregistration — commendable). But as it stands there is no central theorem, a speculative cosmology attached, and no external adjudication. Stated rigor is not demonstrated rigor.
Falsifiability: 6/10. The agency core is genuinely falsifiable by ablation and that is the program's best feature (call it 7–8 in isolation). MaxLove, the phase transition, and the cosmology drag the average down because they're weakly specified.
Nobel-plausibility-if-experiments-succeed: 2/10. Blunt: Nobels reward confirmed physical discovery, not accounting frameworks or simulations. If every notebook succeeds, you have a beautiful theoretical-biology / complexity result — Boltzmann-medal-shaped, not Stockholm-shaped. The only Nobel route is the cosmology branch actually replacing the dark sector, and no deterministic notebook can settle that — it needs Euclid/DESI-class data. So conditioning on "notebook experiments succeed" barely moves the Nobel needle. The thing you'd want to be Nobel-plausible is the thing you should currently be firewalling.

The honest summary: you have one genuinely new operational idea buried under fourteen papers of scaffolding and one credibility-destroying appendage. Extract the break-even theorem, run experiment #1, cut the cosmology loose, and the "interesting" program has a shot at "important."
