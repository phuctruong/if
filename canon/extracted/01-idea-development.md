# IF Theory  
## The Game of Life, Recharged

> An experimental computational framework for studying informational charge, entropy, expansion, memory, reflection, and self-repair in artificial universes.

---

## Overview

**IF Theory**—Informational Field / Informational Battery Theory—is a computational research project that extends Conway-style cellular automata into expandable toy universes.

Each universe begins with simple local rules. From those rules, we investigate whether larger phenomena can emerge:

- structure,
- entropy,
- expansion,
- persistent memory,
- self-repair,
- computation,
- replication,
- cooperation,
- parasitism,
- and reflective behavior.

The central question is:

> **What if Conway’s Game of Life began as a Big Bang and evolved structures that learned to recharge themselves?**

IF Theory does not claim to reproduce the physical universe or prove a new theory of cosmology. It provides a programmable environment in which informational-cosmology ideas can be expressed precisely, simulated, measured, challenged, and potentially falsified.

---

## The Core Idea

Conway’s Game of Life demonstrates that surprisingly complex structures can emerge from extremely simple rules.

IF Theory asks what happens when a cellular universe also contains:

1. **Informational charge**  
   A cell’s capacity to perform work, preserve organization, or influence its neighborhood.

2. **Entropy**  
   Local disorder, uncertainty, incompatibility, or loss of usable informational structure.

3. **Phase**  
   A cell’s internal orientation or state relative to nearby cells.

4. **Memory**  
   Compressed information about a cell’s previous states and interactions.

5. **Reflection**  
   The ability of a stable structure to use memory to alter future behavior.

6. **Expansion**  
   The ability of the simulated universe to create new space when activity reaches its boundary.

The purpose is not to install complexity manually. The purpose is to discover the smallest rules under which complex behavior appears spontaneously.

---

## Scientific Status

IF Theory is currently a **toy computational framework** and an exploratory research program.

It may be useful for studying questions such as:

- Can expansion change the lifetime of organized structures?
- Can local memory improve persistence?
- Can reflective behavior emerge from stability?
- Can self-repairing structures evolve?
- Under what conditions does cooperation outperform extraction?
- Are there critical transitions between dead, frozen, chaotic, living, and reflective universes?
- Can persistent IF structures perform universal computation?
- Which quantities, if any, remain invariant as the board expands?

The project distinguishes three levels of claim.

### Supported computational claim

> Particular local rules produce particular measurable behaviors in the simulator.

### Interpretive claim

> Those behaviors may provide useful analogies for entropy, emergence, biological organization, cognition, or cosmology.

### Unsupported claim

> The simulator proves that the real universe follows the same rules.

The third claim is explicitly outside the scope of the project.

## Design Principles

### 1. Minimal rules first

The initial model should use as few primitive variables and update equations as possible.

Complexity should be earned through emergence, not inserted through elaborate rules.

### 2. Measurement before interpretation

Every important concept must correspond to a computable quantity.

Terms such as *charge*, *entropy*, *reflection*, *life*, and *recharge* require operational definitions.

### 3. Search rule space

No single rule set should be treated as sacred.

The project will simulate thousands of parameter combinations and classify their outcomes.

### 4. Reproducibility

Every published figure should be reproducible from:

- a configuration file,
- a random seed,
- a versioned simulator,
- and a Jupyter notebook.

### 5. Scientific humility

The simulator should reveal where the theory fails as clearly as where it succeeds.

### 6. Moral behavior must emerge

Cooperation, care, and MaxLove will not initially be hard-coded as universal laws.

Instead, later experiments will test whether agency-preserving cooperation becomes more stable, productive, or resilient than parasitism under specified conditions.

## The IF Cell

The initial IF cell contains four primary state variables:

\[
x_i(t) = \left(q_i,e_i,\phi_i,r_i\right)
\]

where:

- \(q_i\) = informational charge,
- \(e_i\) = local entropy,
- \(\phi_i\) = phase or orientation,
- \(r_i\) = reflection capacity.

Additional derived variables may include:

- activity,
- local alignment,
- stability,
- age,
- memory depth,
- boundary pressure,
- neighborhood complexity,
- and resource flow.

The first implementation should keep all primary variables bounded:

\[
q_i,e_i,r_i \in [0,1]
\]

and phase may be represented as:

\[
\phi_i \in [0,2\pi)
\]

or, in the simplest discrete model:

\[
\phi_i \in \{-1,+1\}
\]

The discrete version should be implemented first because it is easier to inspect and falsify.

## IF Life v0.1

For each cell \(i\), define local phase alignment:

\[
A_i(t)=
\frac{1}{|\mathcal{N}_i|}
\sum_{j\in\mathcal{N}_i}
\frac{1+\cos\left(\phi_j-\phi_i\right)}{2}
\]

so that:

\[
0\leq A_i\leq1
\]

A provisional update system is:

\[
q_i(t+1)=
\operatorname{clip}
\left[
q_i(t)
+\alpha A_i(t)
-\beta e_i(t)
-\mu a_i(t),
0,1
\right]
\]

\[
e_i(t+1)=
\operatorname{clip}
\left[
e_i(t)
+\gamma\left(1-A_i(t)\right)
-\delta r_i(t)
+\xi_i(t),
0,1
\right]
\]

\[
r_i(t+1)=
\operatorname{clip}
\left[
r_i(t)
+\eta\,\sigma_i(t)
-\kappa r_i(t),
0,1
\right]
\]

where:

- \(\alpha\) controls charge gained through alignment,
- \(\beta\) controls charge lost through entropy,
- \(\mu\) is the cost of activity,
- \(\gamma\) controls entropy generated by misalignment,
- \(\delta\) controls entropy reduction associated with reflection,
- \(\xi_i\) represents noise or perturbation,
- \(\eta\) controls reflection gained through stability,
- \(\kappa\) represents memory or reflection decay,
- \(\sigma_i\) is a measurable local stability score.

A cell is provisionally active when:

\[
q_i(t)-e_i(t)>\theta_A
\]

These equations are hypotheses, not axioms. They exist to provide a minimal executable starting point.

## Preventing Free Recharge

A major danger is accidentally creating charge from nothing.

Therefore, later versions should distinguish between:

- **stored charge**,
- **transferred charge**,
- **usable free energy**,
- **waste entropy**,
- and **external or boundary input**.

Reflection should not simply delete entropy without cost.

A more physically disciplined model may require:

\[
\Delta E_{\text{local}}<0
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
\Delta E_{\text{environment}}>0
\]

Reflection would then reorganize or export entropy rather than annihilate it.

The project will compare:

1. nonconservative exploratory rules,
2. globally conservative rules,
3. open universes with external input,
4. and expanding universes whose new cells contain specified initial resources.

## The Expandable Universe

Classic cellular automata normally operate on a fixed or periodic board.

IF Theory allows the domain to expand.

Let boundary pressure be:

\[
B(t)=
\frac{1}{|\partial\Omega_t|}
\sum_{i\in\partial\Omega_t}a_i(t)
\]

where \(\partial\Omega_t\) is the current boundary.

When:

\[
B(t)>\theta_B
\]

for a sustained number of steps, the simulator adds a new region around the current grid:

\[
\Omega_t\rightarrow\Omega_{t+1}
\]

Several expansion laws will be compared:

- threshold expansion,
- constant-rate expansion,
- activity-proportional expansion,
- charge-funded expansion,
- stochastic expansion,
- and locally expanding space.

Expansion must have an explicit accounting rule. New space cannot silently introduce unlimited energy unless that is the declared experimental condition.

## The Big Bang Seed

The first cosmological experiment begins with a highly symmetric, highly charged state:

\[
q_i(0)=1
\]

\[
e_i(0)=0
\]

\[
\phi_i(0)=\phi_0
\]

A small perturbation breaks the symmetry:

\[
\phi_k(0)=\phi_0+\epsilon
\]

or:

\[
e_k(0)=\epsilon
\]

The simulation then measures whether the perturbation:

- disappears,
- propagates,
- fragments the universe,
- generates persistent structures,
- triggers expansion,
- creates oscillations,
- or produces higher-order organization.

This is called a **Big Bang seed** as a computational metaphor. It is not presented as a literal model of the physical Big Bang.

## Global Metrics

The first simulator will track at least the following quantities.

### Total charge

\[
Q(t)=\sum_i q_i(t)
\]

### Total entropy

\[
E(t)=\sum_i e_i(t)
\]

### Total reflection

\[
R(t)=\sum_i r_i(t)
\]

### Active population

\[
P(t)=\sum_i \mathbf{1}\left[q_i(t)-e_i(t)>\theta_A\right]
\]

### Mean alignment

\[
\bar{A}(t)=\frac{1}{N_t}\sum_i A_i(t)
\]

### Occupied area

\[
V(t)=|\Omega_t|
\]

### Expansion rate

\[
H_{\mathrm{IF}}(t)=
\frac{V(t+1)-V(t)}{V(t)}
\]

This is an internal simulation metric and is not equivalent to the cosmological Hubble parameter.

### Structural persistence

Track connected structures across time and measure:

- lifetime,
- size,
- shape retention,
- movement,
- replication,
- and recovery after perturbation.

### Still-water score

A provisional composite measure is:

\[
S(t)=
w_Q\tilde{Q}(t)
-w_E\tilde{E}(t)
+w_R\tilde{R}(t)
+w_P\tilde{P}(t)
\]

where each term is normalized.

Because arbitrary composite scores can conceal bad assumptions, all component metrics must also be reported separately.

## Universe Classification

Each simulation will be classified into one or more regimes.

### Dead universe

Activity falls below a minimum threshold and does not recover.

### Frozen universe

The universe reaches a nearly static configuration with little novelty.

### Chaotic universe

Local states remain highly unstable and persistent structures are rare.

### Explosive universe

Activity or expansion grows without a meaningful bound.

### Oscillatory universe

The system repeatedly cycles through recognizable macrostates.

### Living universe

Persistent structures maintain boundaries, process resources, or reproduce.

### Reflective universe

Some structures use accumulated state information to alter their behavior and improve persistence or repair.

### Cooperative universe

Resource-sharing structures outperform isolated or purely extractive structures under a specified fitness measure.

These names describe computational regimes, not biological or metaphysical proof.

## Initial Experiments

### Experiment 1: Fixed versus expandable space

**Question:** Does board expansion increase the lifetime or diversity of persistent structures?

- a fixed board,
- a periodic board,
- and an expandable board.

### Experiment 2: Reflection ablation

**Question:** Does reflection increase structural persistence?

Compare:

- \(r_i=0\),
- passive accumulated memory,
- and active reflection with a defined cost.

### Experiment 3: Critical recharge threshold

**Question:** Is there a transition point above which reflective structures survive?

Sweep \(\delta\), \(\eta\), and \(\kappa\).

### Experiment 4: Perturbation recovery

**Question:** Can stable structures repair themselves?

Damage a percentage of each persistent structure and measure recovery.

### Experiment 5: Cooperation versus extraction

Introduce transferable charge and compare strategies that:

- retain resources,
- share resources,
- conditionally reciprocate,
- steal resources,
- or punish repeated extraction.

Measure:

- individual lifetime,
- lineage lifetime,
- total population,
- resource inequality,
- structural diversity,
- and global persistence.

### Experiment 6: Rule-space phase diagram

Sweep the primary parameters:

\[
(\alpha,\beta,\gamma,\delta,\eta,\kappa)
\]

and map the regions associated with dead, frozen, chaotic, explosive, living, and reflective outcomes.

## Falsifiable Questions

The project becomes scientifically useful only when it can fail.

Initial falsifiable hypotheses include:

### H1

Expandable grids produce longer-lived structures than fixed grids under at least some resource-conserving rule families.

### H2

Reflection improves recovery from perturbation only when its informational benefit exceeds its energetic cost.

### H3

There is a critical parameter region separating transient memory from persistent reflective organization.

### H4

Unconditional cooperation is vulnerable to extraction, while conditional cooperation can become stable under repeated local interaction and limited migration.

### H5

Most randomly sampled IF rule sets do not produce reflective universes.

A negative result for any hypothesis is a valid result.

## Philosophical Motivation

IF Theory begins with an intuition:

> A universe may be understood not only by what matter it contains, but by how usable organization is stored, discharged, transferred, remembered, and restored.

A highly ordered initial state resembles a charged informational battery. Symmetry breaking creates differences. Differences enable interaction. Interaction creates history. History enables memory. Memory may eventually enable reflection.

The strongest philosophical possibility is not that intelligence defeats entropy without cost. It is that intelligence becomes increasingly capable of preserving meaningful organization while paying, redirecting, and accounting for that cost.

Within later IF experiments, **MaxLove** will refer to a proposed strategy class:

> Preserve and increase agency by helping systems recharge without unnecessarily destroying the agency of other systems.

Whether that strategy succeeds is an experimental question inside the model, not a predetermined conclusion.

## How novel is it really?

### 1. The universe as computation is not novel

The idea that the physical universe could be a cellular automaton or discrete computational system goes back through Konrad Zuse, Edward Fredkin, Stephen Wolfram and, in a more technically developed form, Gerard ’t Hooft’s cellular-automaton interpretation of quantum mechanics. ### 2. Information as physically fundamental is not novel

Wheeler’s “it from bit,” the holographic principle, quantum-information reconstructions of physics, and constructor theory all give information a foundational physical role. The holographic principle in particular connects information bounds with spacetime geometry, while constructor theory attempts to formulate physical laws in terms of possible and impossible information transformations. citeturn820329search6turn891768academia38turn891768academia37

### 3. Order emerging through entropy production is not novel

Prigogine’s theory of dissipative structures established that organized, stable structures can arise in open systems far from thermodynamic equilibrium. This is important because life does not maintain order by reversing the second law; it maintains local organization through energy flows while producing entropy in its environment.
