# The Agency Threshold (IF-H1) and the Artificial Universe Lab

> Layer: SCIENCE. Distilled from msgs [20][37][49] + Paper 5 (when harvested). The highest-probability original result in the program.

## The lab

Resource-conserving artificial universes (CA / causal graphs / Lenia-class
active matter) with: local states, local interactions, limited energy,
transition costs, mutable connectivity. **No teleological primitives.**
Agents are *detected* (persistent bounded structures identified automatically),
then *audited* (causal-work ablations).

## The experiment family

| Notebook | Hypothesis | Falsifier |
|---|---|---|
| `04_if_causal_work_threshold` | Π_A ≈ 1 is a phase boundary (discontinuity, critical slowing, hysteresis, universality) | smooth linear scaling everywhere → agency is a gradient |

| `15_arrow_of_time` | time direction emerges where independently recoverable records accumulate asymmetrically under reversible rules | arrow appears only via initial-condition/update-order selection |

| `18_repair_mortality` | self-repair evolves only in intermediate damage window d_min < d < d_max; mortality emerges when indefinite repair costs more than replacement | repair monotone in damage, or purely reward-designed |
| `19_functional_consciousness` | global counterfactual workspace emerges only under action-competition (conflicting sensors, exclusive actions, delayed consequences); ablating global sharing selectively kills planning/counterfactuals/self-report-like behavior | equivalent performance from purely local control |

## Discipline specific to this lab

- **Functional claims only.** Nothing here bears on phenomenal experience.
  The phrase "consciousness" appears only as "functional consciousness" and
  the firewall is stated in every doc that uses it.
- **Universality or bust:** a threshold seen in one rule family is a
  curiosity; the claim requires ≥3 unrelated families (rung 274177).
- **Divergence experiments** (panel round 1): actively hunt cases where IF
  disagrees with KW semantic information and with FEP — if IF never disagrees
  with either, it is a repackaging, and we say so in the kill log.
