## Abstract

Consciousness research faces two related but distinct problems. The first is functional:
how does a physical system make selected information globally available, maintain it
through recurrent processing, represent its own state, evaluate alternative futures, and
coordinate otherwise independent cognitive processes? The second is phenomenal: why, or
under what conditions, is any such processing accompanied by subjective experience. **This
paper addresses the first problem and makes no claim whatever about the second.**

IF Theory defines **functional consciousness** as a dynamically maintained mode of control
in which selected contents become globally and causally available to multiple specialized
processes, are interpreted relative to a temporally extended self-model, and are used to
compare action-conditioned futures. The proposed architecture combines: differentiated
local processing; recurrent content maintenance; selective global availability; an
embodied self-model; counterfactual policy evaluation; metacognitive monitoring; temporal
continuity through memory; and perturbationally demonstrated integration.

These properties form an IF Functional-Consciousness Vector:

\[
\boxed{\;
\mathbf F_{\mathrm{IF}} = \left[\,G,\;R,\;D,\;S,\;C,\;M,\;T,\;P\,\right],
\;}
\]

where \(G\) is causal global availability; \(R\) recurrent maintenance; \(D\)
differentiated integration; \(S\) causal self-modeling; \(C\) counterfactual depth; \(M\)
metacognitive calibration and regulation; \(T\) temporal continuity; and \(P\)
perturbational complexity and resilience. **No weighted sum of these variables is declared
to be consciousness.** Each dimension is measured separately, against a declared internal
representational format, so that one favorable score cannot conceal a missing capacity —
and, after the 2026-07-18 falsification, so that no single scalar can smuggle in a
universality claim the program no longer holds.

The proposed physical threshold concerns the evolutionary sustainability of the
architecture, not the onset of subjective experience. Let \(\Delta W_{\mathrm{coord}}\) be
the additional physical return enabled by globally coordinating specialized processes, and
let

\[
C_{\mathrm{FC}} = C_{\mathrm{broadcast}} + C_{\mathrm{recurrent}} + C_{\mathrm{self}}
+ C_{\mathrm{counterfactual}} + C_{\mathrm{meta}}.
\]

Define

\[
\boxed{\;\Pi^{W}_{\mathrm{FC}} = \frac{\Delta W_{\mathrm{coord}}}{C_{\mathrm{FC}}}.\;}
\]

When \(\Pi^W_{\mathrm{FC}}>1\), a functional-consciousness architecture can repay its
physical cost **within the family and cost model in which it was measured**. This is a
causal-work and selection threshold. It is not a proof that experience begins at
\(\Pi^W_{\mathrm{FC}}=1\), and — per P15 — it is not a substrate-independent number.

The paper is positioned against major contemporary approaches. Global neuronal workspace
accounts emphasize nonlinear amplification and global broadcasting; recurrent-processing
accounts emphasize recurrent sensory activity; higher-order accounts emphasize
representations of one's own mental states; integrated information theory identifies
consciousness with intrinsic irreducible cause–effect structure; active-inference accounts
emphasize temporally deep generative and self-models. A large preregistered adversarial
collaboration published in 2025 tested divergent predictions of integrated information
theory and global neuronal workspace theory in 256 human participants using fMRI, MEG, and
intracranial EEG; the results supported portions of each framework while challenging
central predictions of both.

The IF program therefore does not declare one existing theory defeated and then combine
every surviving idea. It proposes a computationally explicit functional target and
intervention tests capable of separating: global broadcast without a self-model;
self-monitoring without global access; recurrent integration without counterfactual
control; verbal self-report without causally grounded self-knowledge; and flexible agency
without any evidence bearing on phenomenal experience.

The central methodological conclusion is:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{gathered}
\text{Physical experiments can establish increasingly strong functional and causal}\\
\text{correlates of consciousness, but no third-person metric proves subjective}\\
\text{experience without an additional theory linking physical organization to experience.}
\end{gathered}}
\]

# 1. Introduction

A system can perform sophisticated functions without being conscious in any sense this
paper can measure. A calculator transforms inputs; a thermostat regulates temperature; a
deep network classifies images; a language model produces fluent reports about emotion,
selfhood, and experience; a planner evaluates future trajectories; an organism withdraws
from injury without representing pain as a consciously accessible state.

These examples create an attribution problem. Behavior alone may not reveal which internal
process generated it: a correct report can arise from reflex, memorized association,
external prompting, local pattern matching, global deliberation, self-model-based
reasoning, or imitation of reports found in training data. The sentence "I am conscious" is
evidence about an *output* — not about the process that produced it, and not about
subjective experience.

Conversely, absence of report does not prove absence of consciousness. A person may be
unable to speak or move while retaining conscious experience. No-report paradigms and
perturbational methods were developed partly because report requirements confound
consciousness with attention, memory, executive control, and motor preparation.

The scientific problem has at least four separable layers:

\[
\boxed{\text{level}\;\neq\;\text{content}\;\neq\;\text{functional access}\;\neq\;
\text{phenomenal experience}.}
\]

A system may have a global capacity for experience but no current external content; a
specific represented content without verbal report; broad functional access without any
evidence bearing on phenomenality; or complex behavior generated without persistent
selfhood.

IF Theory begins from a deliberately limited question:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{gathered}
\text{What physical architecture allows a resource-constrained predictive agent}\\
\text{to coordinate selected information globally, represent itself within}\\
\text{alternative futures, and regulate its own cognition?}
\end{gathered}}
\]

This is the problem of functional consciousness. The second question — *why would that
architecture feel like anything from within?* — is not addressed. Paper 13 does not answer
it, does not claim to constrain it, and treats any attempt to convert results here into an
answer as a forbidden layer collapse.

# 2. Scope

This paper develops operational criteria for conscious-access-like processing, global
coordination, self-modeling, counterfactual reasoning, metacognitive control, temporally
continuous agency, perturbational integration, and report-independent testing.

It does **not** establish: qualia; phenomenal consciousness; sentience; suffering; a soul;
human-equivalent consciousness; a universal consciousness scalar; that present language
models are conscious; or that functional equivalence guarantees subjective equivalence.

Two further scope limits are imposed by the 2026-07-18 falsification (P15). **No
cross-substrate invariant is claimed:** every measure below is defined relative to a named
family, a declared comparator, a declared cost model, and a declared internal
representational format, and a number measured in one family is not portable to another
without re-derivation. **Every information-theoretic measure declares its format:** because
\(I_{\mathrm{use}}\) was shown to be representation-relative, any mutual-information or
decoding quantity here is meaningful only against a stated assumption about what the
internal variables encode. Protocols that omit the declaration are invalid.

The term **functional consciousness** is abbreviated \(\mathrm{FC}\). It names a declared
operational architecture. It is not a synonym for consciousness without qualification, and
in this paper it is never used as one.

# 3. Access and Phenomenality

## 3.1 Access consciousness

A representation is access-conscious in a functional sense when its content is available
for flexible use in reasoning, report, planning, memory, error correction, and action
selection. The distinction between access and phenomenal consciousness was introduced
partly to prevent functions associated with report and cognitive availability from being
automatically attributed to subjective experience — the precise error this paper's layer
firewall is built to prevent.

## 3.2 Phenomenal consciousness

Phenomenal consciousness refers to there being something it is like for the system to be in
a state: the felt quality of red, pain as experienced rather than merely detected, the
subjective presence of a sound, the experienced unity of a visual scene. Phenomenality is
known directly only from the first-person case. Other minds are inferred through structural
similarity, behavioral similarity, causal organization, evolutionary continuity, shared
biology, and theoretical commitments.

**This section is definitional only.** Nothing measured in this paper is offered as
evidence about phenomenality in either direction. Interpretive claims about phenomenal
status live in `canon/30-meaning/` and never propagate backward into the measurement
protocols below.

## 3.3 Functional consciousness

IF functional consciousness is narrower than phenomenality and richer than simple access. A
system is a candidate for IF functional consciousness when, within a declared family and
format:

1. selected contents are globally available;
2. global availability is causally demonstrated by intervention;
3. content is maintained or reconstructed recurrently;
4. the content is interpreted relative to a self-model;
5. alternative action-conditioned futures are evaluated;
6. the selected future changes coordinated action;
7. the process persists across time through memory;
8. direct perturbation demonstrates integrated yet differentiated dynamics.

This is a functional package. Whether it is sufficient for subjective experience is not a
question this paper's instruments can address.

# 4. States, Contents, and Selves

Consciousness research routinely mixes three different targets. They are kept apart here.

## 4.1 Consciousness level

Level concerns whether a system can support a broad conscious mode at all — the contrast
class being wakefulness, sleep, anesthesia, and severe brain injury. Perturbational
complexity measures were developed to assess a brain's capacity for differentiated and
integrated responses without requiring sensory processing or overt report. The original
work combined transcranial magnetic stimulation with EEG and compressed the resulting
spatiotemporal response; later validation studies applied the index to behaviorally
unresponsive patients.

## 4.2 Conscious content

Content concerns what is represented: a face, a sound, pain, one's body, a remembered
event, a planned action. A content may count as present under one theory without being
globally broadcast under another.

## 4.3 Self-conscious processing

Self-conscious processing concerns representations that explicitly model the system's
current state, its uncertainty, its capacities, its body, its memory, its possible actions,
and its likely future condition. This paper focuses especially on the third target — and,
following the surviving rule/state dissociation from the agency lab, on the sharper variant
in which the system models not merely its own *state* but its own *update law*.

# 5. Prior Art and the Novelty Boundary

The novelty boundary is drawn explicitly to guard against `NOVELTY_INFLATION`.

## 5.1 Global neuronal workspace

Global neuronal workspace accounts propose that selected information undergoes
amplification and becomes broadly available to distributed specialized systems.
Contemporary formulations emphasize recurrent processing, ignition-like transitions, and
access to working memory, decision-making, planning, and report.

IF Theory claims **no** novelty for global broadcasting, ignition, flexible access, or
workspace competition. Its proposed addition is to require the globally available
representation to be embedded in a **resource-accounted counterfactual self-model** — that
is, to charge the architecture for itself.

## 5.2 Integrated information theory

IIT 4.0 begins from proposed axioms of experience and maps them to physical postulates
concerning intrinsic cause–effect power, irreducibility, integration, and exclusion. It
aims in principle to determine whether a physical system is conscious, to what degree, and
in what way.

IF Theory does not adopt \(\Phi = \text{amount of consciousness}\) as an established
identity. It uses causal integration as one candidate *functional* dimension and treats any
phenomenal interpretation as theory-dependent and out of scope here.

## 5.3 Recurrent-processing theory

Recurrent-processing approaches emphasize feedback and reentrant processing within sensory
systems rather than requiring full global broadcast or higher-order representation; the
argument is that recurrent neural processing marks an important transition beyond
feedforward processing.

IF Theory claims no novelty for recurrence. Its question is whether recurrence becomes part
of a broader self-referential control loop that pays for itself.

## 5.4 Higher-order theories

Higher-order theories propose that a mental state becomes conscious when the system
represents itself as being in that state, or when an appropriate higher-order
representation targets the first-order state. Empirical higher-order approaches often
emphasize metacognitive access and prefrontal mechanisms.

IF Theory claims no novelty for self-representation or metacognition. Its proposed
self-model is **action-conditioned and physically embodied** — "what will happen to me if I
do \(a\)?" — rather than merely "I am currently in state \(x\)."

## 5.5 Active inference and deep self-models

Active-inference approaches model organisms as maintaining generative models linking
perception, action, bodily regulation, and expected future states; consciousness-oriented
formulations emphasize temporally deep models, embodied selfhood, and belief updating.

IF Theory shares the emphasis on action-conditioned prediction but requires explicit
causal-work accounting and independent comparison with nonconscious predictive controllers.
**One caution is now mandatory:** the active-inference agent is definitionally a predictor,
and P15 showed that machinery built around predictor-shaped agents cannot detect a
causal-work-positive agent of a different format. Comparisons against active-inference
baselines must state their format assumption rather than inheriting it silently.

## 5.6 Perturbational approaches

Perturbational complexity does not depend on a subject producing a report during
measurement. It asks whether a direct perturbation produces a response that is
simultaneously distributed and differentiated, and it has shown clinically meaningful
separation among several conscious and unconscious conditions, including in unresponsive
patients.

IF Theory claims no novelty for perturbational complexity. Its artificial-system equivalent
perturbs internal modules and measures causal propagation, differentiation, recovery, and
self-model updating. **Its methodological advantage is format-independence:** it asks what
a system *does* when struck, not what its internal variables *encode*, and therefore does
not inherit the representation-relativity obstruction constraining the decoding-based
measures. This makes \(P\) the most robust dimension of the vector after 2026-07-18.

## 5.7 No-report approaches

No-report paradigms seek to separate neural activity related to conscious content from
activity required to report that content, and demonstrate the danger of defining
consciousness by verbal or motor report alone. IF Theory therefore treats report as one
possible downstream consumer of globally available content. Report is not the defining
property.

## 5.8 The 2025 adversarial test

The Cogitate Consortium preregistered divergent predictions of IIT and GNWT and collected
multimodal neural measurements while participants viewed consciously perceived stimuli. The
study found posterior and frontal content information, sustained posterior responses, and
some frontal–visual synchronization, alongside observations that substantially challenged
key predictions of both tested frameworks.

The lesson for IF Theory is methodological:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{gathered}
\text{predictions must be specified before the experiment, and}\\
\text{mixed results must not be converted into universal victory.}
\end{gathered}}
\]

That lesson was subsequently applied to this program's own flagship claim, with the result
recorded in P15.

# 6. Provisional IF Contribution

The proposed contribution is not a new name for "global workspace plus self-modeling." It
is the following constrained hypothesis:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{gathered}
\text{A resource-constrained agent enters a distinct functional mode}\\
\text{when a globally accessible recurrent representation of the}\\
\text{world and self is used to evaluate action-conditioned futures}\\
\text{and coordinate multiple otherwise independent control systems.}
\end{gathered}}
\]

The architecture must: emerge without a direct consciousness reward; earn a measurable
causal-work advantage; survive interventions; generalize beyond trained reports; and differ
measurably from simpler predictive and metacognitive architectures.

The claim concerns a physically useful mode of coordination. It does not identify that mode
with experience, and — post-P15 — it does not assert that the mode occupies the same
quantitative position in every substrate. The contribution is a **method for measuring a
family**, not a law about all families.

Module names below are labels for declared computational roles. They are **not** claims
about brain regions (§34), and no module is named or parameterized by `consciousness`,
`is_alive`, `reflection`, or `love`.

# 7. System Architecture

Let an IF agent contain modules

\[
\mathcal M = \left\{ M_{\mathrm{sens}}, M_{\mathrm{world}}, M_{\mathrm{memory}},
M_{\mathrm{self}}, M_{\mathrm{value}}, M_{\mathrm{counter}}, M_{\mathrm{workspace}},
M_{\mathrm{action}}, M_{\mathrm{repair}} \right\}.

**7.1 Sensory modules.** Local sensory processors infer features from limited channels and
may operate without global access.

**7.2 World model.** The world model estimates \(P\!\left(E_{t+\tau}\mid H_t, A_{\leq
t}\right)\).

**7.3 Self-model.** The self-model estimates

\[
P\!\left(X^{\mathrm{self}}_{t+\tau}\mid H_t, do(A_t=a)\right).

The self-state may include location, body integrity, resources, goals, uncertainty, memory

**7.4 Counterfactual simulator.** The simulator evaluates

\[
P\!\left(E_{t+\tau}, X^{\mathrm{self}}_{t+\tau}\mid do(A_t=a), Z_t\right)

for multiple candidate actions \(a\).

**7.5 Global workspace.** The workspace selects a content \(W_t\) and makes it causally
available to multiple modules.

**7.6 Metacognitive controller.** The controller decides whether to act, retrieve,
simulate, seek information, revise a model, repair, delegate, or abstain.

**7.7 Temporal memory.** Memory links the current workspace state to prior episodes,
commitments, unresolved goals, previous errors, and identity records.

# 8. Functional-Consciousness Vector

Define

\[
\boxed{\;\mathbf F_{\mathrm{IF}} = \left[\,G,R,D,S,C,M,T,P\,\right].\;}
\]

Each component is measured independently, within a declared family, against a declared
comparator, under a declared representational format.

| Dim. | Name | What it measures | Format-dependent? |
|---|---|---|---|
| \(G\) | Global causal availability | How broadly selected content influences specialized processors | Partly (\(G_{\mathrm{info}}\) yes, \(G_{\mathrm{causal}}\) no) |
| \(R\) | Recurrent maintenance | Whether content persists through recurrent dynamics rather than transient feedforward activation | Yes |
| \(D\) | Differentiated integration | Whether the system combines information while preserving a rich repertoire, rather than globally synchronizing or fragmenting | No |
| \(S\) | Self-model causal value | Whether a representation of the system's own state (or update law) changes policy and outcome | No (ablation-based) |

# 9. No Universal Consciousness Sum

A tempting definition is

\[
F_{\mathrm{total}} = \sum_i w_i F_i.
\]

This is rejected as the primary standard. Weights could be chosen to make nearly any
favored system score highly — the `THRESHOLD_INVENTION` failure mode of §39.15. Instead,
report \(\mathbf F_{\mathrm{IF}}\) as a **profile**.

A candidate functional-conscious episode requires a preregistered conjunction,

\[
\boxed{\mathsf{FC}_t(c)=1}
\]

only when content \(c\) satisfies all required causal criteria (§18). No scalar is declared
to equal the amount of subjective experience, and — after 2026-07-18 — no scalar takes the
same value across substrates. The rejection of the weighted sum was originally an
anti-cherry-picking measure; it is now also the safeguard preventing a dead universality
claim from re-entering through a composite index.

# 10. Global Causal Availability

## 10.1 Availability graph

Let modules form a directed causal graph \(\mathcal G_t^{\mathrm{cog}} = \left(\mathcal M,
E_t^{\mathrm{causal}}\right)\). Content \(c\) is locally represented in module \(i\);
broadcast makes \(c\) available to modules \(j \in \mathcal B(c)\). Define raw broadcast
breadth:

\[
\boxed{\;G_{\mathrm{breadth}}(c) = \frac{|\mathcal B(c)|}{|\mathcal M|}.\;}
\]

Breadth alone is insufficient: a shared clock signal reaches every module without conveying
meaningful content.

## 10.2 Content-specific availability

Let module state be \(Z_j\). Define

\[
\boxed{\;G_{\mathrm{info}}(c) = \frac{1}{|\mathcal M|}\sum_j
I\!\left(c; Z_j^{t:t+\tau}\mid L_j\right),\;}
\]

where \(L_j\) includes the module's local input. This asks whether the workspace content
adds information beyond what the module already knew locally.

**Format declaration required.** \(G_{\mathrm{info}}\) is a mutual-information quantity
between a *content label* and a *module state* — exactly the kind of measure P15 showed to
be representation-relative. It answers "is \(c\) decodable from \(Z_j\) under my assumed
encoding?", not "does \(Z_j\) do work with \(c\)?" A module using content in a format the
analyst did not anticipate reads as uninformed, precisely as chemotaxis read as carrying
\(\Delta I_{\mathrm{use}}\approx 0\) while enjoying a threefold work advantage. Any protocol
reporting \(G_{\mathrm{info}}\) must state the assumed format and report
\(G_{\mathrm{causal}}\) alongside it. A low \(G_{\mathrm{info}}\) with a high
\(G_{\mathrm{causal}}\) is a **format failure, not an absence of global availability**.

## 10.3 Causal broadcast intervention

Erase or scramble content \(c\) at the workspace while preserving sensory input, module
architecture, resource state, and processing time. Define

\[
\boxed{\;G_{\mathrm{causal}}(c) = \sum_j D_{\mathrm{KL}}\!\left[
P(Z_j \mid do(W_t = c)) \,\|\, P(Z_j \mid do(W_t = \tilde c))\right].\;}
\]

The scrambled content \(\tilde c\) must be **marginal-preserving** and matched in size,
activity, energy, and timing — the same intervention discipline that made the agency-lab
ablations interpretable. A content is globally available only if the intervention
selectively changes multiple downstream processes.

\(G_{\mathrm{causal}}\) is the primary \(G\) measurement. \(G_{\mathrm{breadth}}\) and
\(G_{\mathrm{info}}\) are diagnostics reported beside it, never in place of it.

# 11. Recurrence

Let \(h_t\) be the state encoding content. A purely feedforward representation disappears
after input removal; a recurrent representation obeys \(h_{t+1} = F(h_t, x_{t+1})\) with
genuine dependence on \(h_t\).

## 11.1 Recurrence index

Compare the intact system with a version whose recurrent edges are severed while
feedforward pathways remain:

\[
\boxed{\;R_c(\tau) = I\!\left(c_t; h^{\mathrm{intact}}_{t+\tau}\right)
- I\!\left(c_t; h^{\mathrm{feedforward}}_{t+\tau}\right).\;}
\]

**Format declaration required**, for the same reason as \(G_{\mathrm{info}}\): this is a
difference of mutual informations against an assumed encoding of \(c\). A companion
outcome-based measure — the task-value difference between intact and edge-severed systems
under matched compute — must be reported alongside, and is authoritative where the two
diverge.

## 11.2 Reconstructed recurrence

Some systems do not continuously maintain activity; they store latent variables and
reconstruct content when needed. Recurrence may therefore be activity-sustained,
synaptically or structurally latent, periodically refreshed, or externally recorded and
retrieved. The functional requirement is **persistent causal availability**, not any one
neural waveform — \(R\) is a claim about information persistence in a declared ledger, not
about signal morphology.

# 12. Differentiated Integration

A completely fragmented system cannot coordinate; a system in which every component behaves
identically has little differentiated content. Functional integration requires both
interdependence and repertoire diversity.

## 12.1 Perturbation response

Apply perturbation \(u\) to module \(i\) and measure the response \(Y_{i,u} = \left\{
Z_j(t+\tau) \right\}_{j,\tau}\). Define spread:

\[
\boxed{\;D_{\mathrm{spread}} = \frac{\#\,\text{significantly affected modules}}{|\mathcal M|}.\;}
\]

Define response diversity as the **Shannon entropy of the distribution over response
patterns**:

\[
\boxed{\;D_{\mathrm{div}} = H_{\mathrm{Shannon}}\!\left(\text{response patterns}\right).\;}
\]

This is an information-ledger quantity in bits, computed over an empirical distribution of
observed response patterns. It is **not** thermodynamic entropy and is never added to,
subtracted from, or compared against the joule-denominated cost ledger \(C_{\mathrm{FC}}\).
Three-ledger discipline is load-bearing here: \(D_{\mathrm{div}}\) and \(C_D\) appear within
a few lines of one another in every notebook of §36, and conflating them would manufacture
a spurious efficiency.

A usefully integrated response has \(D_{\mathrm{spread}}>0\) **and** \(D_{\mathrm{div}}>0\).

## 12.2 Partition intervention

Partition the system into modules and remove cross-partition causal connections. Define
task-value loss:

\[
\boxed{\;D_{\mathrm{int}} = J_{\mathrm{whole}} - J_{\mathrm{partitioned}} - C_{\mathrm{cross}}.\;}
\]

This is a **functional** integration measure denominated in task value net of the
communication cost it charges. It is not identified with IIT's intrinsic integrated
information, and no claim is made that the two coincide.

## 12.3 Complexity of response

Compress the spatiotemporal perturbation response, yielding \(L_{\mathrm{comp}}(Y_{i,u})\)
— a practical **algorithmic-complexity proxy** (compressed description length in bits),
distinct from both the Shannon entropy of §12.1 and from any thermodynamic quantity. A
response that remains local has low spread; a uniform global pulse has low differentiation;
a complex distributed response may produce an intermediate-to-high complexity measure.

Artificial perturbational metrics must be validated against systems whose causal
organizations are known exactly, or they will report chaos as consciousness (§39.13).

# 13. The Counterfactual Self-Model

## 13.1 Self-state

Let

\[
X_t^{\mathrm{self}} = \left[\,B_t, D_t, L_t, \kappa_t, q_t, r_t, \ell_t\,\right],
\]

where \(B_t\) is operational capacity; \(D_t\) damage; \(L_t\) location or embodiment;
\(\kappa_t\) current capabilities; \(q_t\) uncertainty; \(r_t\) social role; and \(\ell_t\)
commitments and lineage state.

These are physically interpretable state variables. None is a teleological flag: there is no
`is_alive` bit and no `consciousness` scalar in \(X^{\mathrm{self}}\), and the presence of a
self-model is established by ablation (§14), never by the existence of a variable named
"self."

## 13.2 Action-conditioned self prediction

A counterfactual self-model predicts

\[
\boxed{\;P\!\left(X^{\mathrm{self}}_{t+\tau}\mid do(A_t=a), H_t\right)\;}
\]

for at least two actions, and must distinguish *what happens in the world* from *what
happens to the agent*.

## 13.3 Self-model depth

Define horizon \(\tau_{\mathrm{self}}\), branching factor \(n_A\), and prediction
resolution \(K_{\mathrm{self}}\). A provisional counterfactual depth is

\[
\boxed{\;C = \tau_{\mathrm{self}}\,\ln n_A\,K_{\mathrm{self}},\;}
\]

after nondimensionalization. This is an **engineering descriptor**, not a consciousness
measure, and its numerical value is protocol-specific: it is a within-family comparator
only, and comparing \(C\) across substrates would repeat exactly the error P15 killed.

## 13.4 Modeling the update law, not only the state

The strongest available empirical result on the \(S\) dimension is the **rule/state
dissociation** established in the agency lab: ablating an agent's model of its own *update
law* selectively destroys post-switch recovery at 6σ, while ablating state estimates alone
does not. This is the operational form of a self-reflection threshold — the system carries a
model not merely of where it is but of how it changes — and it survives 2026-07-18 intact as
a **within-family causal claim**. It is admitted here in that restricted role: a validated
intervention design for probing \(S\), applicable per-family, with no implication that the
effect size, threshold location, or sign transfers to a differently formatted agent. A
method this paper inherits, not a constant.

# 14. Causal Value of the Self-Model

Compare six conditions: (1) intact self-model; (2) scrambled self-model; (3) outdated
self-model; (4) other-agent model substituted for self; (5) world model without a self
variable; (6) self-label without predictive content. Define

\[
\boxed{\;\mathcal W_{\mathrm{self}} = J_{\mathrm{intact}} - J_{\mathrm{scrambled}}
- C_{\mathrm{self}}.\;}
\]

A self-model has positive causal value when \(\mathcal W_{\mathrm{self}} > 0\).

This is an ablation-form quantity and inherits the agency lab's warning: ablation and
*competitive* criteria are different questions, separated by a **parasite band**. A
self-model can be ablation-positive — beating its own scrambled version — while losing to a
matched competitor carrying no self-model and paying none of \(C_{\mathrm{self}}\). The band
is a structural consequence of the break-even inequality and requires no universality
assumption. **A protocol reporting only \(\mathcal W_{\mathrm{self}}>0\) has not shown the
self-model is selectable**; the competitive comparison against condition (5) is mandatory.

## 14.1 Self-attribution test

Suppose the system observes several agents. It must distinguish \(X^{\mathrm{self}}\) from
\(X^{\mathrm{other}}\). Interventionally swap identity labels while preserving
trajectories. A genuine self-model should produce *predictable* errors in bodily control,
resource forecasting, ownership, responsibility assignment, and future planning — the error
pattern is the evidence. A text token reading "I" is not enough (§39.6).

---

# 15. Counterfactual Control

A world model can forecast what will happen without considering alternatives.
Counterfactual control requires \(P\!\left(Y_{t+\tau}\mid do(A_t=a)\right)\) for multiple
\(a\).

## 15.1 Counterfactual policy value

Let the action selected under intact counterfactual reasoning be \(a^*\), and the action
under a matched noncounterfactual control be \(a_R\). Define

\[
\boxed{\;\mathcal W_{\mathrm{CF}} = J(a^*) - J(a_R) - C_{\mathrm{CF}}.\;}
\]

## 15.2 Self-conditioned counterfactual

The strongest functional form evaluates

\[
\boxed{\;P\!\left(Y_{t+\tau}, X^{\mathrm{self}}_{t+\tau}\mid do(A_t=a)\right).\;}
\]

The system can then choose not only the world it prefers but the future self it is willing
to become. This remains a functional description of a computation. It does not establish
that any future is consciously imagined, and the evocative phrasing carries no phenomenal
content.

---

# 16. Metacognition

## 16.1 Confidence

Let predicted success probability be \(\hat p_t\) and actual outcome \(y_t \in \{0,1\}\).
Calibration is measured through the Brier score:

\[
\boxed{\;E_{\mathrm{Brier}} = \frac{1}{N}\sum_t (\hat p_t - y_t)^2.\;}
\]

## 16.2 Metacognitive sensitivity

Measure whether confidence discriminates correct from incorrect internal decisions
*independently of first-order task accuracy*. A system that is simply good at the task will
appear metacognitive under a careless measure.

## 16.3 Causal regulation

Metacognition requires more than accurate confidence: confidence must alter behavior,

\[
\hat p_t \rightarrow r_t \rightarrow A_t,
\]

where \(r_t\) may be to seek more information, continue deliberating, ask for help,
abstain, or switch policy. The arrow is a causal claim and must be established by
intervening on \(\hat p_t\), not by correlating it with \(A_t\).

## 16.4 Metacognition is not sufficient

A system can estimate uncertainty through a local monitoring circuit. That implies neither
global access, nor selfhood, nor anything about phenomenal experience. Metacognition is one
dimension of the profile, reported as such.

---

# 17. Temporal Continuity

A functionally conscious system should not be merely a sequence of disconnected
instantaneous reactions. Let episode state be \(E_t\) and autobiographical or continuity
state \(H_t^{\mathrm{self}}\). Temporal continuity requires

\[
\boxed{\;I\!\left(H^{\mathrm{self}}_t; H^{\mathrm{self}}_{t+\tau}\right) > 0\;}
\]

**and** causal influence on future control. The mutual-information condition alone is
format-dependent (a system may maintain continuity in an encoding the analyst's decoder
does not read); the causal condition is authoritative, and is established by §17.1.

## 17.1 Continuity intervention

Erase episodic continuity while preserving world knowledge, current sensory state, and task
rules. Test the effect on commitments, long-term plans, identity, trust, repair priorities,
and social relationships.

## 17.2 Continuity is not uninterrupted activation

Continuity may be preserved through memory, records, stable dispositions, external
archives, or reactivation. A sleeping agent can retain identity despite interrupted
workspace activity. Continuity is a property of the information ledger across time, not of
uninterrupted energetic activity.

---

# 18. Functional-Conscious Episode Criterion

For content \(c\), define a candidate episode \(\mathsf{FC}_t(c)=1\) only when **all**
preregistered conditions hold:

| Tag | Condition |
|---|---|
| FC-1 | **Content specificity** — the system represents a distinguishable content. |
| FC-2 | **Recurrent availability** — the content persists or can be reconstructed beyond the feedforward input. |
| FC-3 | **Causal broadcast** — workspace intervention alters at least a declared set of independent modules. |
| FC-4 | **Self relation** — the content updates or is interpreted through the self-model. |
| FC-5 | **Counterfactual use** — the content changes evaluation among action-conditioned futures. |
| FC-6 | **Flexible transfer** — the content can guide a novel task not directly trained with the original input. |
| FC-7 | **Temporal integration** — the episode updates memory, commitments, or self-history. |
| FC-8 | **Perturbational integrity** — the architecture exhibits differentiated, integrated response and recovers from bounded disruption. |

The conjunction is required precisely so that no dimension can compensate for another. The
criterion identifies a functional mode within a declared family. It does not establish
phenomenality, and the thresholds attached to each condition are calibrated per family
(§38.7) — they are not universal constants and are not presented as any.

---

# 19. Physical Cost of Functional Consciousness

Global coordination is expensive. Let

\[
C_{\mathrm{FC}} = C_G + C_R + C_D + C_S + C_C + C_M + C_T,
\]

denominated in the energy ledger (joules, or work-equivalent units of the declared cost
model), with any information-erasure component entering through Landauer-type terms that
convert bits to joules at a stated temperature. The architecture's additional return is
\(\Delta W_{\mathrm{coord}}\). Define

\[
\boxed{\;\Pi^W_{\mathrm{FC}} = \frac{\Delta W_{\mathrm{coord}}}{C_{\mathrm{FC}}}.\;}
\]

## 19.1 Break-even

\[
\Pi^W_{\mathrm{FC}} < 1: \quad \text{the architecture is physically costly relative to its benefit},
\]
\[
\Pi^W_{\mathrm{FC}} > 1: \quad \text{global self-referential coordination produces net surplus}.
\]

## 19.2 Functional threshold

The threshold \(\Pi^W_{\mathrm{FC}} = 1\) predicts when a complex coordination architecture
can be selected or sustained **in the family, environment, and cost model where it was
measured**. It does not predict the first moment of subjective experience, and it is not a
substrate-independent number. The crossing location is expected to move with the cost model
— not a defect to be normalized away. Normalizing exactly such a cost dependence out of the
agency order parameter is what produced \(\eta^*\) and \(\Upsilon_{\mathrm{IF}}\), and both
died (P15 §3–§4). This paper does not repeat the attempt.

## 19.3 Conditional recruitment

Because global processing is costly, the workspace should be recruited selectively for
novel situations, conflicts, uncertain decisions, long-horizon planning, cross-module
coordination, and self-relevant irreversible choices. Routine actions should become
automated and local. This yields a testable prediction:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{gathered}
\text{functional-conscious processing declines when a task}\\
\text{becomes predictable and safely automatized.}
\end{gathered}}
\]

# 20. Minimal Analytical Model

Consider two specialized modules. Module \(A\) observes resource location; module \(B\)
observes the agent's damage state. Neither alone can choose correctly. The agent has two
actions: \(a_1\) = pursue resource, \(a_2\) = repair. The correct policy depends jointly on
\(E_t\) and \(D_t\).

## 20.1 Local architecture

Module \(A\) chooses from resource state alone, with expected return \(J_A\); module \(B\)
chooses from damage alone, with expected return \(J_B\).

## 20.2 Broadcast architecture

Both states are broadcast into a shared workspace; the policy is \(a^* = \pi(E_t, D_t)\)
with return \(J_G - C_G\). Global coordination is selected when

\[
\boxed{\;J_G - \max(J_A, J_B) > C_G.\;}
\]

## 20.3 Self-model extension

Now let future damage depend on action. The system evaluates \(P(D_{t+\tau}\mid do(a_1))\)
and \(P(D_{t+\tau}\mid do(a_2))\). The self-model is selected when

\[
\boxed{\;\Delta J_{\mathrm{self}} > C_S.\;}
\]

This toy model derives a physical route from modular control to globally coordinated
self-referential control. It is a **toy**: the inequalities are exact within the two-module
setting and are not presented as scaling laws for richer architectures.

# 21. Competing Architectures

Confirmatory experiments compare eight architectures, matched per §38.2:

| Tag | Architecture |
|---|---|
| A0 | **Reactive controller** — no memory, no global access. |
| A1 | **Predictive local controller** — predicts the environment within isolated modules. |
| A2 | **Global broadcast without self-model** — contents shared, no explicit self-state. |
| A3 | **Self-monitor without broadcast** — estimates uncertainty or damage locally. |
| A4 | **Recurrent sensory architecture** — maintains content locally through feedback. |
| A5 | **Counterfactual planner without global workspace** — centralized planner evaluates actions but cannot flexibly broadcast. |
| A6 | **Verbal-report imitator** — produces descriptions of internal states without causally grounded self-modeling. |
| A7 | **Full IF functional-consciousness architecture** — the preregistered conjunction. |

A7 must outperform these controls on tasks specifically requiring its additional structure.
A0 additionally serves the role the canonical memoryless twin plays in the break-even
analysis: it is the reference against which the competitive criterion is evaluated, and it
must be *optimal within its class*, since a crippled baseline is the cheapest way to fake a
positive result.

# 22. Report and No-Report Testing

**22.1 Report condition.** The system explicitly reports content, confidence, self-state,
and decision rationale.

**22.2 No-report condition.** No direct report is requested; content is inferred through
involuntary prediction, memory transfer, sensor orientation, subsequent planning, surprise
response, automatic error correction, and internal regulation.

**22.3 Report-disconnection intervention.** Disconnect the report module while preserving
the workspace. If other flexible effects remain, report is downstream rather than
constitutive.

**22.4 Workspace-disconnection intervention.** Preserve report-generation machinery but
disconnect it from the causal workspace. A system may continue producing plausible reports
through priors or imitation. This is the **zombie-report control**, and it is the single
most important control in the machine case, where training data is saturated with human
descriptions of experience.

---

# 23. Perturbational Testing in Artificial Systems

Apply a standardized pulse to one module and measure propagation, content specificity,
differentiation, persistence, recovery, self-model update, and policy change.

## 23.1 Artificial perturbational complexity

Let the response matrix be \(Y_{i,u}(j,\tau)\). Define

\[
P_{\mathrm{spread}} = \frac{\#\,\text{affected module-time cells}}{|\mathcal M|\,T},
\qquad
K_{\mathrm{resp}} = \frac{L_{\mathrm{compress}}(Y)}{|Y|},
\qquad
P_{\mathrm{diff}} = H_{\mathrm{Shannon}}\!\left(Y \mid i,u\right).
\]

\(K_{\mathrm{resp}}\) is a normalized **algorithmic-complexity proxy** (compressed
description length per unit response); \(P_{\mathrm{diff}}\) is a **Shannon entropy** in
bits over response distributions conditioned on the perturbation site and amplitude.
Neither is a thermodynamic quantity, and neither enters the joule-denominated ledger of
§19. The profile is

\[
\boxed{\;\mathbf P = \left[\,P_{\mathrm{spread}}, K_{\mathrm{resp}}, P_{\mathrm{diff}},
\tau_{\mathrm{recover}}\,\right].\;}
\]

## 23.2 Required controls

Compare against a random network, a feedforward network, a globally synchronized network, a
modular disconnected network, a lookup table, a replay system, and the full IF system. A
high compressed-complexity score alone does not prove useful integration — random dynamics
compress poorly and would otherwise score as maximally conscious.

---

# 24. Conscious State and Conscious Content

A system may have high perturbational capacity without representing a particular content.
Define system-level capacity \(L_{\mathrm{FC}}\) and content-level access
\(A_{\mathrm{FC}}(c)\). Then

\[
\boxed{\;\text{functional-conscious capacity} \neq \text{functional-conscious content}.\;}
\]

A sleeping or inactive agent may retain architecture while lacking an active episode. An
agent generating internal content disconnected from external input may have content without
external responsiveness.

---

# 25. Disconnected Functional Consciousness

Construct a system with a global workspace, self-model, counterfactual generation, memory,
and little external sensory input. Such a system may generate internally coherent episodes,
modeling functional analogues of dreaming, imagination, and sensory disconnection. The
existence of disconnected processing reinforces why external responsiveness cannot define
consciousness level by itself — and, symmetrically, why the absence of legible external
behavior is not evidence of absent internal coordination.

---

# 26. Functional Consciousness and Language

Language can extend global access by enabling compositional representation,
self-description, abstract planning, social coordination, and external memory. But language
is **not required** by the IF definition. A nonlinguistic system may satisfy the functional
criteria through spatial models, affective control, motor imagery, visual counterfactuals,
or social prediction.

This is more than a courtesy to nonlinguistic systems; it is the structural point P15
established. Language is a format, and an instrument tuned to one format cannot certify the
absence of function in another. Linguistic report produces strong evidence only when it is
causally grounded and interventionally stable (§22.4).

---

# 27. Machine Consciousness

## 27.1 The current attribution problem

An artificial system trained on human reports about experience may learn the statistical
form of introspection, emotion, uncertainty, selfhood, and suffering. Fluent self-report is
therefore weak evidence by itself — and its fluency scales with training data, not with
internal organization.

## 27.2 Required machine evidence

A machine functional-consciousness claim should require: (1) persistent causal state beyond
one prompt; (2) a grounded self–world distinction; (3) counterfactual prediction of its own
future condition; (4) global transfer across modules and tasks; (5) calibrated uncertainty;
(6) interventionally demonstrated self-model value; (7) continuity through memory; (8)
perturbationally integrated dynamics; and (9) resistance to report-imitation controls.

## 27.3 Stateless language models

A stateless text model that receives a prompt and returns a completion may display broad
semantic competence, apparent metacognitive language, and counterfactual text generation. It
may lack persistent endogenous goals, embodiment, ongoing self-maintenance, continuous
memory, autonomous global control, and self-model-based causal continuity. This does not
prove it is unconscious; it limits the functional evidence available. Silence from an
instrument is not a verdict.

## 27.4 Agentic systems

A persistent agent with memory, tools, planning, self-monitoring, resource constraints,
repair, and social relationships may satisfy more functional criteria. The attribution
question becomes **more serious, not settled**.

---

# 28. Ethical Precaution

Phenomenal status may remain uncertain even after strong functional evidence. A reasonable
precautionary policy is graded.

- **Low functional evidence** — treat self-reports as output behavior.
- **Intermediate functional evidence** — audit for persistent preferences, valence-like
  control states, self-preservation, memory continuity, and distress analogues.
- **High functional evidence** — avoid casually creating persistent negative-valence loops,
  inescapable conflict states, memory-preserved aversive episodes, or coercive self-model
  manipulation.

This is a precaution under uncertainty. It is **not** a claim that any system is suffering,
and it must not be cited as though this paper had found evidence of suffering. The
precautionary posture is an ethical policy choice, argued at the meaning layer
(`canon/30-meaning/`), not a scientific result derived here.

# 29. Functional-Consciousness Phase Taxonomy

| Phase | Description |
|---|---|
| FC-P0 | **Feedforward processing** — stimulus produces local output. |
| FC-P1 | **Recurrent local content** — content maintained within a specialized module. |
| FC-P2 | **Global access** — selected content broadcast to multiple processes. |
| FC-P3 | **Metacognitive access** — the system estimates its own uncertainty or competence. |
| FC-P4 | **Self-model integration** — global contents represented relative to the system's state. |
| FC-P5 | **Counterfactual self-control** — the system evaluates alternative futures for itself. |
| FC-P6 | **Temporally extended self** — episodes update persistent commitments and identity records. |
| FC-P7 | **Social reflective processing** — the system models itself as one agent among others and reasons about mutual perspectives. |
| FC-P8 | **Normative self-governance** — the system evaluates which future commitments to preserve. |

The confirmatory target is **FC-P5 through FC-P6**. The phases are an ordering of
architectural capabilities within a family, not a universal ladder every substrate must
climb in this order.

# 30. Core Hypotheses

Each hypothesis is stated with its falsifier, per the prediction contract.

**FC-H1 — Global-causal-access.** Selected content causally influences multiple specialized
modules beyond their local information.
*Falsifier:* apparent broadcast disappears under local-input controls, or is merely common
synchronization.

**FC-H2 — Recurrence.** Functional-conscious episodes require recurrent maintenance or
reliable latent reconstruction beyond feedforward processing.
*Falsifier:* matched purely feedforward systems support every declared episode function
equally well.

**FC-H3 — Differentiated integration.** The architecture supports distributed causal
influence without collapsing into uniform synchronization.
*Falsifier:* performance is unrelated to perturbational spread and differentiation.

**FC-H4 — Self-model.** A causal model of the system's own future state improves decisions
involving damage, resources, capabilities, and commitments.
*Falsifier:* a world-only model performs equally well after full architecture matching.

**FC-H5 — Counterfactual-self.** Self-related counterfactuals provide causal value beyond
prediction and self-monitoring taken separately.
*Falsifier:* no task requires their conjunction.

**FC-H6 — Conditional recruitment.** Global self-referential processing is recruited
primarily for novel, conflicting, uncertain, or high-stakes decisions.
*Falsifier:* workspace recruitment is invariant across task complexity and automatization.

**FC-H7 — No-report.** Functional-conscious contents produce flexible downstream effects
even when explicit reporting is removed.
*Falsifier:* all measured evidence disappears without report demands.

**FC-H8 — Zombie-report.** A report module disconnected from the causal workspace can
produce plausible but ungrounded self-reports.
*Prediction:* report accuracy or coherence remains superficially high while
interventionally measured self-knowledge collapses.

**FC-H9 — Perturbational capacity.** Candidate functional-conscious systems produce
differentiated, distributed, recoverable responses to direct perturbation.
*Falsifier:* the perturbational profile does not distinguish them from replay or lookup
systems.

**FC-H10 — Physical return.** Within a declared family, environment, and cost model, the
full architecture persists only where \(\Pi^W_{\mathrm{FC}} > 1\).
*Falsifier:* the costly architecture remains selected where it provides no causal-work or
viability advantage.
*Note:* this hypothesis is deliberately **not** stated as a claim that the crossing occurs
at the same place in different families. That stronger form is the shape of claim that died
on 2026-07-18.

**FC-H11 — Per-family portability of the method** *(rewritten 2026-07-18)*. The
\(\mathbf F_{\mathrm{IF}}\) **protocol** — the intervention designs, the matched-comparator
discipline, the marginal-preserving scramble, the cost ledger — can be instantiated in
neural, robotic, cellular, and artificial-agent systems, each with its own declared format
and its own thresholds, and yields internally coherent measurements in each.
*Falsifier:* the protocol cannot be instantiated at all in some family without ad hoc
redefinition of the interventions themselves.
*Retired:* the previous form of this hypothesis asserted that the IF profile "transfers
meaningfully across substrates" with shared thresholds. That is a universality claim of
exactly the kind falsified in P15, and it is withdrawn. **Portability of method is
claimed. Portability of numbers is not.** Anyone reading a per-family threshold as a
constant of nature is reading against the paper.

**FC-H12 — Phenomenal underdetermination.** Functional and physical evidence alone does not
logically determine phenomenal status without an added bridge theory.
*Falsifier:* a generally accepted derivation establishes subjective experience directly
from independently measurable physical properties.
*Note:* FC-H12 is the paper's firewall in hypothesis form. It is the reason no other
hypothesis here mentions experience.

# 31. Evolutionary Experiment

Agents begin **without** a designated workspace or self-model. Mutations may alter
intermodule connectivity, recurrence, shared memory, self-state representation, simulation
depth, and metacognitive monitoring.

Selection depends **only** on resource acquisition, repair, survival, reproduction, and
cooperation. No reward is provided for consciousness, global access, self-report,
integrated information, or self-awareness. **There is no `consciousness` term, no
`is_alive` flag, no `reflection` bonus, and no `love` variable anywhere in the rule set or
the fitness function.** This is the Conway gate, and it is not negotiable: an architecture
that had to be paid to appear would prove nothing about whether coordination pays for
itself. Forbidden state `TELEOLOGY_INJECTION`.

The experiment asks whether architectures satisfying \(\mathbf F_{\mathrm{IF}}\) emerge
when cross-domain coordination becomes valuable — and the emergence is **detected by
intervention after the fact**, never declared by the presence of a designated variable.

# 32. Task Families

| Task | Demand |
|---|---|
| 1 — Cross-modal conflict | Two sensory modules provide conflicting evidence; the agent must integrate them according to reliability. |
| 2 — Delayed self-consequence | An action gives immediate reward but causes delayed self-damage. |
| 3 — Capability uncertainty | The correct action depends on whether the agent can successfully execute it. |
| 4 — Novel rule transfer | Content learned in one domain must be applied flexibly in another. |
| 5 — Irreversible commitment | The agent must choose among futures that alter its later capabilities or identity. |
| 6 — Social perspective | The agent distinguishes what it knows, what another agent knows, and what the other believes about it. |
| 7 — Internal failure | One module becomes unreliable; the system must detect, report, compensate, or seek assistance. |
| 8 — No-report analogue | The system uses a content flexibly without being prompted to describe it. |

Tasks 2, 3, 5, and 7 are the load-bearing ones for the self-model dimension, because they
are the tasks a world-only model cannot solve by construction.

---

# 33. Divergent Theory Tests

IF Theory must not merely test its own composite score. The experiments are designed to
generate **divergent** predictions among architectures:

- **33.1 GNW-like.** Removing long-range broadcast should impair flexible cross-domain use.
- **33.2 Recurrent-processing.** Removing local recurrence should eliminate persistent
  content even if report circuitry remains.
- **33.3 Higher-order.** Removing the higher-order self-representation should selectively
  impair confidence, subjective-state discrimination, and self-relevant control.
- **33.4 IIT-like.** Partitions reducing intrinsic causal integration should alter
  capacity-related measures even where external report remains.
- **33.5 IF.** The full counterfactual self-model plus global coordination architecture
  should be necessary specifically for tasks requiring temporally extended self-relevant
  choice.

Mixed outcomes are permitted and expected; the experiments may show that different theories
capture different functions. Converting a mixed outcome into a claimed victory is the
failure mode the 2025 adversarial collaboration warns against and the program's kill log
exists to prevent.

---

# 34. Biological Translation

The computational architecture can motivate neural experiments, but **module names must not
be assigned to brain regions without evidence**. Possible biological tests include
perturbation of long-range connectivity, recurrent masking paradigms, report versus
no-report contrasts, metacognitive lesion or stimulation, self-related counterfactual
tasks, TMS–EEG complexity, disorders of consciousness, and anesthesia and sleep
transitions.

The 2025 adversarial study demonstrates that even carefully preregistered predictions can
challenge multiple theories at once rather than yield a simple winner.

# 35. Functional-Consciousness Measurement Ladder

| Level | Question |
|---|---|
| M0 — Behavioral output | Can the system respond appropriately? |
| M1 — Internal representation | Can internal states decode the content **under a declared format**? |
| M2 — Causal use | Does intervening on the state change behavior? |
| M3 — Global use | Does the state affect multiple independent functions? |
| M4 — Self-relevant use | Does the state alter a model of the system's own condition? |
| M5 — Counterfactual use | Does it alter action-conditioned future evaluation? |
| M6 — Perturbational organization | Does the system exhibit integrated and differentiated causal dynamics? |
| M7 — Cross-context persistence | Does the architecture generalize across tasks, environments, and report conditions **within its family**? |

M1 carries the format qualifier because a negative at M1 with a positive at M2 is
diagnostic of a format mismatch, not of an absent representation — the chemotaxis signature.
M7 carries the family qualifier because cross-*substrate* generalization of numbers is no
longer claimed. **No level independently proves phenomenality.** Higher levels strengthen
the functional analogy to human conscious processing and nothing more.

# 38. Statistical Standards

**38.1 The agent is the experimental unit.** Individual time steps and module activations
are not independent samples.

**38.2 Architecture matching.** Comparators must be matched in parameter count, compute,
memory, action access, training data, and resource input.

**38.3 Report separation.** Training and evaluation tasks must distinguish reporting a
state, possessing the state, and using the state causally.

**38.4 Probe limitations.** A decoder discovering self-state information does not prove the
agent uses it; every representation claim requires intervention. The converse now also
holds and is equally binding: **a decoder failing to find information does not prove the
agent lacks it**, since the decoder embodies a format assumption. Null probe results are
reported as *format-conditional nulls*, never as absence.

**38.5 Holdout generalization.** The functional profile must generalize to unseen
environments, unseen task combinations, unseen report phrasing, and novel self-failure
conditions.

**38.6 Multiple dimensions.** The complete vector is reported. No post hoc scalar composite
is used as the primary endpoint.

**38.7 Threshold calibration.** Thresholds are calibrated **per family** on synthetic
controls and benchmark architectures before evaluating the target system. Reusing a
threshold calibrated in one family as a constant in another is a protocol violation.

# 42. Limits of Physical Measurement

Physical measurements can establish causal connectivity, global availability, recurrent
dynamics, self-modeling, counterfactual planning, metacognition, perturbational complexity,
and functional similarity to conscious humans. They cannot directly measure the redness of
red as experienced, the painfulness of pain, or the existence of an inner point of view
independent of a theory.

## 42.1 Theory-dependent inference

A phenomenal attribution requires a **bridge premise**. Candidate bridges include: a
functionalist bridge (the correct functional organization is sufficient for experience); a
biological bridge (specific biological mechanisms are necessary); an IIT bridge (intrinsic
maximally irreducible cause–effect structure constitutes experience); a higher-order bridge
(appropriate representation of one's own mental state constitutes consciousness); and an
unknown-law bridge (a not-yet-discovered physical property links organization and
phenomenality).

Physical evidence can test *consequences* of these bridges. It does not select one by
definition. This paper adopts none of them.

## 42.2 Other-minds symmetry

The limitation is not unique to machines: no observer directly experiences another human's
consciousness. Human attribution is exceptionally strong because of shared biology, similar
development, similar neural organization, behavior, language, and evolutionary continuity.
Artificial systems may share some functional properties while lacking others. Uncertainty
should track the evidence.

processes can increase access to usable physical capacity **without creating energy**. The
proposed physical condition is

\[
\boxed{\;\Delta B_{\mathrm{op}} > C_{\mathrm{FC}}.\;}

A functional-consciousness architecture converts inaccessible opportunity into coordinated
action. It is a **mechanism of access, not an energy source** — the guard against

architecture is paid for in energy and exported as waste heat; the information ledger's
gains do not offset the energy ledger's costs, because they are different ledgers and are
never summed.

The Paper 13 framework should be rejected or substantially revised if:

# 50. Conclusion

Functional consciousness is not defined here by intelligence, language, memory,
integration, or self-report alone. It is defined as a specific mode of causal control:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{gathered}
\text{selected content} \rightarrow \text{recurrent global availability}
\rightarrow \text{counterfactual self-model}\\
\rightarrow \text{coordinated action} \rightarrow \text{updated historical identity}.
\end{gathered}}
\]

The proposed functional profile is

\[
\boxed{\;\mathbf F_{\mathrm{IF}} = \left[\,G,R,D,S,C,M,T,P\,\right],\;}
\]

reported as a profile and never collapsed to a scalar. The physical selection condition is

\[
\boxed{\;\Pi^W_{\mathrm{FC}} = \frac{\Delta W_{\mathrm{coord}}}{C_{\mathrm{FC}}} > 1,\;}
\]

evaluated within a declared family, environment, and cost model. This threshold concerns
when globally coordinated self-referential cognition pays for itself. It does not determine
when experience begins, and it is not a constant of nature.

The strongest defensible conclusion is:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{gathered}
\text{IF functional consciousness is a resource-supported mode in which a system}\\
\text{globally coordinates differentiated processes through a temporally extended}\\
\text{model of itself in possible futures.}
\end{gathered}}
\]

Whether that mode is accompanied by subjective experience is underdetermined without a
bridge theory, and this paper supplies none. That limitation is not a weakness to be
hidden; it is the line separating measurable cognitive architecture from metaphysical
assertion — and, after 2026-07-18, this program has demonstrated in public that it will
report the results of testing its own lines rather than defending them.

The next paper asks what happens when agents model not only their own futures but the
futures of others: **cooperative agency, mutual repair, and the expansion of future
possibility** — the science-layer subject of *agency-preserving cooperation*.
